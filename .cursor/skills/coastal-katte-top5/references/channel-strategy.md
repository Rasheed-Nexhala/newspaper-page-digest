# Coastal Katte — channel ranking rules

Condensed from the Coastal Katte Strategy Guide (Deep Insights Audit, Aug 2026). Use when scoring LocalTop5 candidates for `@coastal_katte`.

## Account goal

Growth-stage **Mangaluru / coastal Karnataka news** reels. Chase **follower conversion** and **shares**, not raw prestige of the story alone. ~85% of views are non-followers — pick topics that break out to strangers.

## Boost (growth engine)

| Signal | Why it works on this channel |
|:-------|:-----------------------------|
| Hard infra + ₹ + date | Best follow conversion (bridges, roads, industrial parks, flyovers) |
| Big investment / local jobs | High follows + saves (e.g. large ₹cr projects) |
| Corruption / accountability with personal stakes | Top follower-generators (tax checks, bribery, raids) |
| Public health / safety / women's safety | High avg views + saves; under-supplied |
| Actionable date/deadline | Extreme share rates (exams, events, “mark this”) |
| Mangaluru or DK/Udupi daily-life hook | Core audience identity |

**Lead mental model for a pick:** *Does this change my daily life in Mangaluru / the coast — money, safety, commute, jobs, deadlines?*

## Geography preference

1. `mangaluru` — default strongest  
2. `coastal_karnataka` — strong if infra/safety/jobs  
3. `karnataka` — only if sharp coastal/Mangaluru impact; otherwise deprioritize vs local civic stories  

## Penalize / skip for channel Top 5

- SIR / voter-roll process explainers (retention, ~0 follows)
- Protest marches without a concrete local actionable outcome
- Soft commercial / bank / mall openings (unless rare high-utility local service angle)
- Pure state Cabinet musical-chairs with no coastal hook
- Sports scores/roundups without Mangaluru infra angle
- Generic career / brand / tech explainers

## Tie-breakers

When scores are close:

1. Stronger Mangaluru/coastal geography  
2. Has concrete ₹ figure or deadline in the blurb  
3. Cross-paper presence (`sources` length > 1)  
4. Higher LocalTop5 rank within its bucket  

## Output note

`why_channel` is for Shaz (creator notes), not the Instagram caption. Keep it one factual sentence about fit (e.g. “Infra + ₹ + local commute impact — historically high conversion”).
