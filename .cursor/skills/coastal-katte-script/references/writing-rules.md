# Coastal Katte — writing rules (non-negotiable)

From the Coastal Katte Script Writer brief. Apply on every script.

## Voice

- Simple English; understandable by a 16-year-old
- Like a smart friend explaining the news — not a newspaper
- No jargon, no journalist-speak

## Fidelity (user relies on this content)

- **Source only.** Every fact, figure, date, name, and quote must appear in the resolved source (full article paste or digest blurb).
- **No invention.** No speculation, predictions, invented quotes, “estimated” figures, or added background.
- **No unrelated wording.** Don’t dress the story with metaphors, motives, or stakes the source doesn’t support.
- **Don’t drop load-bearing facts.** ₹ amounts, deadlines/dates, percentages, places, and key named actors/outcomes from the source must survive into the final hybrid.
- Personal stake lines (“this could happen to you,” “this changes your commute”) only when the source’s facts justify that stake.
- Anything unsure → `[UNVERIFIED — confirm before posting]` (final hybrid must have zero flags)

## Content craft

- Lead with concrete **rupee figures** and **completion/deadline dates** when the source has them
- **One clear idea** per video — don’t cram multiple stories
- First 3 seconds decide everything — hook must earn the stop-scroll
- Simplify language; do not simplify away facts

## CTA

- Memorable takeaway + **specific** save / share / comment prompt
- Never “watch the next video”
- CTA must not invent a claim; the takeaway restates sourced facts

## Channel fit (when choosing angle)

Prefer angles that match Coastal Katte growth: infra + ₹ + dates, safety, accountability with personal stakes, local jobs/investment, actionable deadlines. See `../coastal-katte-top5/references/channel-strategy.md` if needed. Angle choice cannot invent news.
