### China to curb exports of technologies such as AI, photovoltaics

China on Friday announced draft Announcement No. 48 by its commerce and science ministries proposing to restrict exports of more than 40 technologies in AI, biomedicine and photovoltaics, with 14 proposed for a complete ban—including perovskite and tandem solar-cell manufacturing, malicious-code AI systems, and some cell- and gene-therapy platforms—and 26 for restricted export, including polysilicon engineering for lines of at least 30,000 tonnes a year. The editorial says the Election Commission’s special summary revision in 17 States and Union Territories, a re-enrolment drive using original Form 6 without ancestry declaration, a cap on Form 7 objections, and searchable text-PDF rolls together amount to a corrective for flaws in the Special Intensive Revision.
