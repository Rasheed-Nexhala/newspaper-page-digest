**Headline:** Vande Bharat service set for introduction after ghat trial  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** Anil Kumar Sastry  

**Body:** The stage is set for the introduction of a Vande Bharat (VB) Express service between Bengaluru and Mangaluru after the Research, Design and Standards Organisation (RDSO) under the Ministry of Railways successfully conducted trial runs on the Sakleshpur-Subrahmanya Road ghat section of the Hassan-Mangaluru line from August 12 to 19.

The trial runs were conducted on August 12–14 and 17–19 by an RDSO team from Lucknow with a 20-car VB rake fitted with an Automatic Emergency Brake (AEB) system. The AEB was required to prevent the train from exceeding 30 kmph in the ghat section, as mandated by the Commissioner of Railway Safety for all trains traversing the stretch, which has a steep gradient of 1 in 50.

The South Western Railway's (SWR) Mysuru division, which manages the stretch, is now awaiting the arrival of a 16-car rake fitted with an AEB from the Integral Coach Factory, Chennai. The rake used for the trial was not meant for this route, Mysuru Divisional Railway Manager Mudit Mittal told The Hindu.

On August 18, SWR General Manager P. Ananth travelled on the trial rake between Sakleshpur and Subrahmanya Road to witness the run. However, the final route, timings, and halts for the proposed VB Express between Bengaluru and coastal Karnataka are yet to be declared by the Ministry of Railways.

With the ghat section alone taking two-and-a-half hours to traverse, the VB Express is unlikely to have a quick run between Bengaluru and Mangaluru. Once introduced, the service is expected to take at least eight hours (two-and-a-half hours for the ghats, three-and-a-half hours for Bengaluru-Sakleshpur, and two hours for Subrahmanya Road-Mangaluru), compared to the nine-and-a-half hours taken by conventional trains.

While some demand that the service operate strictly between Bengaluru and Mangaluru, others want it extended to Karwar or Madgaon to cover the entire coastal Karnataka region. There is also talk of merging the existing Mangaluru Central-Madgaon-Mangaluru Central VB Express with the proposed train.

**Headline:** Gram panchayats, urban local bodies owe Mescom ₹305.84 crore: Chairman  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** The Hindu Bureau  

**Body:** Gram panchayats and urban local bodies (ULBs) owe Mescom ₹305.84 crore for supplying power, according to K. Harish Kumar, chairman of the company.

Addressing presspersons here on Saturday evening, Mr. Kumar, a former MLC, said that gram panchayats owe ₹298.19 crore for supplying electricity to streetlights and water supply installations, while urban local bodies are yet to pay ₹7 crore for the same.

He said the company suffered a loss of ₹25.77 crore due to heavy winds and rain between April 1 and August 26 this year. In all, 17,770 electric poles and 496.37 km of power supply lines were damaged during the period. Heavy losses were reported in Chikkamagaluru, Dakshina Kannada, and Shivamogga districts.

The company has approved replacing the existing power supply lines in elephant corridors and forest areas with covered conductors. This project will be taken up shortly in Puttur, Shivamogga, Sagar, Chikkamagaluru, Koppa, and Kundapur areas.

He said that a 33 kV underground power supply line will be laid between Kavoor and Urwa Market. The contract for the project has been awarded and it will be taken up shortly. Laying this underground line will help in supplying uninterrupted power to Mangaluru city limits.

In addition, overhead power supply lines in Ullal, Mannagudda, and Kavoor are being replaced with underground lines. The areas include Ullal city municipality, Kumpala, Bagembila, Kolya, Uchhila, Someshwara, Kudroli Temple Road, Lady Hill Road, PVS Circle, Dongarakery, Bejai, Hat Hill, Daddalakad, Bejai Kapikkad, and Bondel-Kavoor Road.

The company has approved the purchase of 5,450 electric transformers to replace non-functional ones. It has also approved the purchase of 705 non-contact voltage detectors for the safety of linemen. In addition, approval for the purchase of a vehicle to transport an underground cable fault locator device has been given. The company has approved the opening of 14 shops to sell smart meters to consumers for the next five years, the chairman said.

**Headline:** Four get jail terms for defaming SC couple  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** The Hindu Bureau  

**Body:** A court in Udupi sentenced four persons to six months of simple imprisonment after finding them guilty of defaming a couple belonging to a Scheduled Caste (SC) and issuing death threats to them.

The judge sentenced Shobha and her daughter Chaitra, both from Alevoor village, and their friends Bhaskar Shetty and Bharath Ganiga to six months of simple imprisonment on each for an offence under Section 504 of the IPC. He further sentenced the four to six months in jail under Sections 3(1)(b), 3(1)(s), and 3(1)(z) of the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act. Bhaskar and Bharath were sentenced to nine months of simple imprisonment for offences under Section 506 of the IPC read with Section 3(1)(r) of the SC/ST Act. All sentences will run concurrently, the judge said.

In a complaint, Panjima, residing in Alevoor, alleged that Shobha and Chaitra were defaming her and her husband by abusing them over their caste and throwing waste and liquor bottles in front of their house. When the couple questioned the two on December 2019, Shobha and Chaitra continued to hurl casteist abuses at them.

**Headline:** Bilimale laments lack of memorials to Molahalli Shiva Rao, Shrinivas Mallya  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** The Hindu Bureau  

**Body:** Expressing dismay over the lack of permanent memorials for Molahalli Shiva Rao and Shrinivas Mallya, who contributed significantly to the coastal region's development, Kannada Development Authority (KDA) Chairman Purushottama Bilimale on Sunday said the authority is ready to support any initiative in this regard.

Speaking at the release of "Mogedastu Moleyuva Molahalli", a book on cooperative leader the late Molahalli Shiva Rao written by Aravind Chokkady, Mr. Bilimale said Shiva Rao used the cooperative sector to build schools in rural areas. Meanwhile, Shrinivas Mallya helped establish an airport, the National Institute of Technology-Karnataka, Surathkal, and worked towards improving the region's urban areas.

"These two toiled, and therefore we are now in a developed state. They are the eyes of the region, and knowing about them means knowing ourselves. It is sad that there is no work so far in the region to remind people of the contributions of these two stalwarts and others who worked for the region's development," Mr. Bilimale said.

Elaborating on Shiva Rao's contributions, Mr. Bilimale said he travelled across the state to acquire land from willing donors to build government schools. According to data collected by the KDA, 3,200 government schools were built in Karnataka between 1915 and 2024. Sixteen schools established in the Sullia region during Shiva Rao's time have now completed 100 years.

Speaking about the book, Mr. Bilimale commended the author for travelling extensively to meet different people and weaving their thoughts on Shiva Rao into the work.

**Headline:** Hindu groups to hold 'Puttur Chalo' rally today over police handling of clash  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** The Hindu Bureau  

**Body:** With several Hindu organisations calling for a "Puttur Chalo" rally on Monday to express their anguish over the alleged poor handling by the police of the recent clash between two groups outside a photocopying centre, the CPI(M) district unit has urged people not to participate in the rally, which it said was being held "to give a communal colour" to a simple clash between customers and the owners of a copying centre.

The organisations will take out the rally in Puttur town on Monday morning. Starting from Darbe, it will culminate in front of the Venkataramana Temple. The police have given conditional approval for the rally and held a route march in the town on Sunday. To maintain peace, additional police personnel will be deployed. Superintendent of Police K. Arun, in a press release, said seven units of the District Armed Reserve, five units of the Karnataka State Reserve Police, and two units of the Special Action Force would be on duty. Additionally, one additional superintendent of police, one deputy superintendent of police, three police inspectors, 10 sub-inspectors, 14 assistant sub-inspectors, and 139 civil police personnel will be deployed.

Condemning the rally, CPI(M) district secretary Muneer Katipalla, in a statement, said the BJP was trying to give a "communal colour" to the clash and disturb peace in the region. He alleged that the absence of communal clashes in the district over the past year had created unrest in the BJP and Sangh Parivar organisations. The Sangh Parivar was targeting Mr. Arun for succeeding in preventing communal clashes, he said, adding that the rally was being held to create tension and help the BJP gain political mileage. Mr. Katipalla urged the people to understand the real intention of the organisers and defeat their objective.

**Headline:** INTACH hosts vocal concert  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** The Hindu Bureau  

**Body:** The Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage (INTACH) Mangaluru Chapter and Art Kanara Trust organised a Hindustani vocal recital by Neeharika Deraje on Saturday, at the Kodialguthu Centre for Art and Culture, Ballalbagh.

Ms. Deraje was accompanied by Shrivathsa Sharma on the tabla, Medha G. Bhat on the harmonium and Samveda G. Bhat on the tanpura.

A disciple of Pandit Ravikiran Manipal for over 12 years, Ms. Deraje opened the recital with raag Puriya Dhanashree, presenting 'Sooraj Doob Gayori' in vilambit khayal, followed by 'Mora Mora Ree' and 'Tori Jai Jaikartar' in drut khayal. She then presented 'Jai Jai Jai Durge Mata', 'Neeko Lage Maa Darsan' and a tarana in raag Durga.

The recital continued with devotional compositions, including the Kabir bhajans 'Ram Niranjan Nyara Re' and 'Ghat Ghat Me Panchi Bolta', the Bengali bhajan 'Bhubhano Mohini', the vachana 'Nudidare Muttina Hara' and Purandara Dasa's 'Ee Pariya Sobagu'.

**Headline:** Byndoor railway station to be redeveloped at ₹100 crore: MP  
**Section:** MANGALURU  
**Byline:** Anil Kumar Sastry  

**Body:** The Mookambika Road Byndoor Railway Station in Udupi district under Konkan Railway Corporation Ltd (KRCL) will be developed as a full-fledged station at a cost of around ₹100 crore, Shivamogga Member of Parliament B.Y. Raghavendra said.

Mr. Raghavendra was speaking after inspecting development works at the station, which falls under his Lok Sabha constituency, recently. The development will be undertaken under the Mookambika Corridor concept, he said.

He said he had recently submitted a representation to Minister of State for Railways V. Somanna to declare Byndoor as the official station for Kollur. Byndoor MLA Gururaj Gantihole and Byndoor Rail Yatri Sangha president Kunjal Venkatesh Kini were among those present.

Master Plan

Quoting Mr. Raghavendra's proposal to the Ministry, Mr. Kini said the Railway Board had recently approved the station development plan at around ₹100 crore. The plan is inbuilt into the third financial restructuring of KRCL, he told The Hindu.

Besides a modern and spacious station building, Byndoor will be upgraded to a block station with three loop lines and a separate goods shed line. A block station enhances operational efficiency by enabling train crossings. Platforms at the station will get full-length shelters and refurbished flooring. The proposal also includes provision for a coach watering facility, Mr. Kini said.
