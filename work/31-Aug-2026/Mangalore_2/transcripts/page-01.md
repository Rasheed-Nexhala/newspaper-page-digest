Headline: Govt planning senior citizens' association in every locality: Khader
Section: MANGALURU, DHNS
Body: 'Will explore possibility of launching it in M'luru on pilot basis'

The state government is considering setting up senior citizens' associations in every locality to bring elderly people together, on the lines of the Bharat Jodo Yuva Sangh initiative aimed at connecting young people, said Dakshina Kannada District In-charge Minister U T Khader.

Speaking after felicitating achievers at the 'Dhanyata' Senior Citizens' Day programme held at the Prerana Hall of the Red Cross Centenary Building here on Sunday, Khader said the government would explore the possibility of launching the initiative on a pilot basis in Mangaluru.

The programme was organised jointly by the Dakshina Kannada district unit of the Indian Red Cross Society, Rotary International District 3182, Dakshina Kannada Ex-Servicemen's Association, Manthana Ojas Trust and the Dakshina Kannada Working Journalists' Association.

The Minister said the sacrifices, patience and commitment of senior citizens had contributed significantly to building a better society. "We have a major responsibility to pass on this legacy to the next generation. Young people should draw inspiration from the experience, values and ideals of senior citizens," he said.

Rotary District 3181 Governor Satish Bolar inaugurated the programme, while Dakshina Kannada Red Cross Society chairman Shantharam Shetty presided.

Senior citizens, who had made notable contributions in various fields, were felicitated during the programme. The awardees included Vajra Rao, Col Nitteguttu Sharath Bhandary, Radhakrishna, T G Shenoy, Robert Franklin Rego, Katta Bhaskara Rai, Sumangala Prabhakar, Pakruddin Kunil, Aruva Koragappa Shetty, Usha Vishwanath and Satish Rao Idya.

As part of the event, Dr Chandini S conducted an awareness session on oral health, while Praveen Udupa spoke about cybercrime awareness and precautions for senior citizens. A free medical check-up camp was also organised in association with the Yenepoya Ayurvedic Medical College. A free dental check-up camp was conducted in collaboration with the Srinivas Institute of Dental Sciences for the benefit of senior citizens.

Headline: Only 97 accident victims obtain benefits under PM-RAHAT scheme in DK district
Section: MANGALURU, DHNS
Body: Only 97 road accident victims in Dakshina Kannada have availed themselves of treatment under the Centre's PM-RAHAT (Prime Minister's Road Accident Victims' Hospitalisation and Assured Treatment) scheme since its launch in February this year, prompting health officials to step up efforts to create awareness about the cashless treatment initiative.

Officials attributed the low number of beneficiaries mainly to lack of awareness among the public and reluctance among some hospitals to register and implement the scheme.

District Health Officer (DHO) Dr HR Thimmaiah said a meeting of all registered hospitals in the district had been convened to create awareness about the scheme and ensure its effective implementation. Although training had been provided to the representatives of the hospitals, the number of beneficiaries remained low.

"A sensitisation programme will be organised for hospitals to ensure that they understand the scheme and implement it effectively," the DHO said.

During the District Development Coordination and Monitoring Committee (DISHA) meeting, Dakshina Kannada MP Capt Brijesh Chowta directed the DHO to take steps to ensure the effective implementation of the scheme across the district.

The PM-RAHAT scheme provides cashless treatment of up to Rs 1.5 lakh for a maximum period of seven days from the date of a road accident. The benefit is available to all victims injured in road accidents involving motor vehicles, irrespective of whether they belong to the Above Poverty Line (APL) or Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.

Dr Thimmaiah said the health department had identified hospitals that had shown reluctance in implementing the scheme. "Notices would be issued to such hospitals shortly, directing them to provide treatment to eligible accident victims under PM-RAHAT."

Mangalore City South MLA D Vedavyasa Kamath pointed out that despite a large number of road accidents being reported within the limits of the Mangaluru City Police Commissionerate and the Dakshina Kannada Police, the number of victims benefiting from the scheme remained low.

He urged the health authorities to intensify awareness campaigns about the scheme. He also called for greater publicity about the Rs 25,000 incentive offered to citizens, who come forward to save the life of a road accident victim, under the initiative popularly referred to as 'Rah-Veer' or Road Hero. Under the procedure for availing the benefits of the scheme, the hospital treating the accident victim is required to submit a report to the police station having jurisdiction over the location of the accident. The incident must subsequently be digitally authenticated by the police through the Electronic Detailed Accident Report (eDAR) platform within 24 hours of the accident. In life-threatening cases, authentication can be completed within 48 hours.

The DHO said all hospitals registered under the scheme were mandated to provide treatment to eligible accident victims.

Headline: DC asks officials to ensure smooth conduct of Prajaseva Andolana meetings from Sept 5
Section: UDUPI, DHNS
Body: Deputy Commissioner Swaroopa T K said Prajaseva Andolana meetings would be organised at taluk and hobli levels across the district from September 5 to hear public grievances and facilitate their resolution at the local level, said.

Chairing a meeting to discuss preparations for the meetings, she directed officials to take all necessary measures to ensure their effective conduct.

She said meetings would be held at the taluk level on the first and third Saturdays under the chairmanship of the district in-charge minister, in the presence of local MLAs. On the second and fourth Saturdays, meetings would be held at hobli and gram panchayat centres under the chairmanship of the respective local MLAs.

The initiative has been launched to ensure that government schemes and programmes reach eligible beneficiaries and to reduce the need for people to repeatedly visit government offices to get their work done. The meetings would provide a platform for residents to present their grievances and seek solutions locally, she said.

Swaroopa directed field-level officials to visit households 15 days before each meeting and distribute pre-printed application forms related to public grievances.

She instructed officials to provide basic facilities, such as computers, internet connectivity and furniture at the meeting venues and establish department-wise counters. All district-level officers must attend the meetings and respond promptly to public grievances, she said. Departments have also been directed not to schedule any other meetings on the day of the Prajaseva Andolana programme. Major departments should set up exhibition stalls at the venues and provide information about various services and facilities available to the public.

Preparations should also be made to distribute government benefits to beneficiaries during taluk-level meetings chaired by the district in-charge minister, she said.

The DC called for extensive publicity about the meetings to encourage people to participate in large numbers and avail themselves of government schemes and services. She directed all departments to work in coordination to make the initiative successful.

Headline: MLA urges CM to declare Tulu as official language during Cabinet meeting in M'luru
Section: UDUPI, DHNS
Body: Puttur MLA Ashok Kumar Rai has urged Chief Minister D K Shivakumar to declare Tulu as Karnataka's second additional official language during the Cabinet meeting scheduled to be held in Mangaluru.

Rai submitted a memorandum to the chief minister during a meeting urging the government to take a decision on according official status to the Tulu language at the upcoming Cabinet meeting.

The MLA said representations had already been made to the government seeking recognition for Tulu as an additional official language.

He said he had also raised the issue during the previous Assembly session, where the government had assured that the matter would be considered.

The MLA pointed out that a committee had earlier been constituted by the state government to study the criteria and procedures followed for recognising an additional official language. The committee had visited Andhra Pradesh and submitted its report to the government after studying the recognition of additional official languages in the neighbouring State.

He said he had also brought to the government's attention in the Assembly the criteria followed in Andhra Pradesh for granting official language status to a local language. The issue had been discussed extensively in the House on several occasions, he added.

"Tulu has its own script and is one of the five major Dravidian languages. It is also an ancient language. Granting Tulu the status of an additional official language would not impose any significant financial burden on the State government," Rai said in his representation.

Referring to the recognition accorded to additional official languages in states, such as Andhra Pradesh, Bihar and West Bengal, Rai urged the chief minister to take a similar step for Tulu in Karnataka.

Speaking about the demand, Ashok Rai said, "I have urged the chief minister to declare Tulu as the second official language of Karnataka at the Cabinet meeting in Mangaluru. Tulu is one of the five major Dravidian languages and is among the ancient languages. According official status to Tulu has been a long-standing demand of the people of this region."

He recalled that previous chief ministers had assured that steps would be taken to consider official recognition for Tulu, but the promised declaration had not yet been made.

"I am confident that the long-pending demand will finally be addressed at the Cabinet meeting to be held in Mangaluru in the coming days," he said.

Headline: Mescom recruits 395 powermen to ease shortage of field staff
Section: MANGALURU, DHNS
Body: The Mangalore Electricity Supply Company (MESCOM) has recruited 395 powermen, which will significantly ease the shortage of field staff, said Mescom Chairman K Harish Kumar.

The remaining 165 powermen will be recruited through KPTCL shortly, he said. "Most of the power men recruited earlier were from districts in North Karnataka. During D K Shivakumar's tenure as the Energy Minister, a condition was imposed that powermen recruited by the Mescom could not be transferred to other ESCOMs. However, later the condition was withdrawn, following which a large number of powermen were transferred," he said.

Of the 395 powermen recruited recently, only five are from Dakshina Kannada district, while the number of recruits from areas under the Mescom jurisdiction does not even cross 50, he said.

Covered conductors

The Mescom will convert power lines passing through elephant corridors and other forest areas into covered conductor systems, in which electrical wires are provided with protective insulation, Harish Kumar said. The work will be taken up in the Puttur, Shivamogga, Sagar, Chikkamagaluru-1 and Chikkamagaluru-2, Koppa and Kundapura divisions, he said.

To improve the safety of powermen, the Mescom will procure 705 non-contact voltage detectors (NCVDs). In addition, six transformer testing benches will be purchased to check the quality of electrical transformers. A vehicle-mounted underground cable fault detection system will also be procured, allowing the equipment to be transported to locations where it is required, he said.

Measures have also been initiated to lay underground cables in the Ullal, Mannagudda and Kavoor sub-divisions, with work already under way at some locations. The Ullal sub-division will cover Ullal, Someshwar, Kumpala, Moorukatte, Bagambila, Kolya, Uchila and Beribail. The Mannagudda sub-division will cover Newchitra Talkies, Kudroli Temple, Ladyhill Road, PVS, Dongerkery, Bejai, KSRTC Bus Stand, Bejai- Kapikad, Hat Hill and Daddalkad. The Kavoor sub-division will cover Bondel to Kavoor- Airport main road.

Smart meters will benefit both consumers and the Mescom, the chairman said. Four smart meter testing benches will be purchased to test the accuracy and quality of meters. Smart meters have been made mandatory for all new electricity connections, with post-paid smart meters for domestic consumers and prepaid meters for temporary connections. The system enables MESCOM to monitor electricity consumption remotely from its offices, eliminating the need for physical meter readings. Consumers can also track their daily electricity consumption through a mobile app, he said.

Pending bills

He said the Mescom was also facing outstanding electricity bill payments of Rs 305.84 crore from gram panchayats and urban local bodies. "This comprises Rs 298.19 crore from gram panchayats and seven crore from TMCs and CMCs."

The Mescom has recruited 100 security personnel on a contract basis and will purchase 512 computers to strengthen cyber security. It also give approval for procuring C-type wedge connectors to ensure continuous supply of quality power and 5,250 Dolo cut-outs to be procured to ensure uninterrupted power supply. In addition, Mescom has purchased 5,450 electrical transformers and taken measures to construct own buildings for the Kundapura, Kaup, Karkala and Shankaranarayana sub-divisions, he added.

'Rs 25.77 crore loss incurred'

Mescom Chairman Harish Kumar said the Mescom had incurred a loss of Rs 25.77 crore from April 1 to August 26, following pre monsoon and monsoon rains coupled with gusty winds. "Chikkamagaluru, Dakshina Kannnada and Shivamogga districts have reported the highest damage. A total of 17,770 electricity poles and 496.37-km of power lines were damaged during the period."

Headline: Trainee pilot laid to rest
Section: UDUPI, DHNS
Body: The mortal remains of 22-year-old trainee pilot Abhishek Poojary, who was killed in a trainee aircraft crash during a training session in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, were brought to his native place in the Udupi district on Sunday.

His last rites were performed at Kadeberke in Kanajaru village of Hiriyadka amid the presence of the grieving family members and relatives.

Abhishek's mortal remains arrived at the Mangaluru International Airport in Bajpe at around 11 am on Sunday. From the airport, the body was taken to his native home at Kadeberke, where family members, relatives and well-wishers gathered to pay their final respects.

Following the last respects, the funeral was held at Kadeberke. The atmosphere turned sombre as the family bid farewell to the young pilot, whose life was cut short while pursuing his dream of becoming a professional pilot.

According to his family, Abhishek had been undergoing training as a trainee pilot with a private aviation company in Kanpur for nearly two-and-a-half years. He had been working towards obtaining the necessary flying experience and qualifications to pursue a career in aviation.
