Headline: India's power-hitters eye one more run feast, series win
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Press Trust of India, Hyderabad
Body:
(Box: MATCH STARTS AT 7PM)

India's marauding batters would be eyeing another run feast against a West Indian attack that would have to do more than just be defensive when the two sides clash in the potentially series-deciding fourth T20 International here on Wednesday.

India need a win to seal the five-match rubber by going 3-1 up, while the West Indies would be desperate to draw level at 2-2 to keep the series alive.

The battle on ground is once again expected to be decided predominantly by the power-hitters in both sides.

The hosts will bank on a line-up whose top order is in great form. The trio of Sanju Samson, Abhishek Sharma and Ishan Kishan has come good in turns to ensure that the Indian middle order has not been put under too much pressure in the series so far.

If it was skipper Shreyas Iyer, who took the lung-opener from them with his maiden T20I hundred, Sanju Samson and Ishan Kishan proved to be difficult to handle in the second game that the visitors eventually won.

Abhishek smashed 69 off just 30 balls in the third T20I in Indore, striking six fours and five sixes to help India post a winning 223-run total.

The only concern for the home team would be a relative lack of game time for the middle order due to the top three's brilliant run.

The likes of Tilak Varma, Nitish Kumar Reddy and Naman Dhir would have to be up for the challenge in case the top three have an off day, especially in a scenario where the team is chasing a big target.

The West Indies, on the other hand, have been struggling to find a way to contain the Indian juggernaut.

Headline: Teen talent inspires Coventry to emphatic win over Newcastle
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Associated Press, Coventry (England)
Body:
Teenager Sidiki Cherif scored one and made one as Coventry overcame a dishevelled Newcastle 4-1 and earned successive Premier League wins on Monday.

The win was a real boost for Coventry, which had lost its first four games of the season but edged past Nottingham Forest in the final league match before the international break.

The victory also marked its first in the Premier League in 25 years after the Midlands club returned to the top division in the summer.

Cherif was brought down just four minutes into the game and Jack Rudoni made no mistake from the spot.

Newcastle controlled most of the first half without troubling the Coventry goal but it was a different story in the second period as the home side won the game with some clinical finishing.

Cherif made it 2-0 with a low shot from the edge of the box three minutes into the second period, and although Sven Botman reduced the deficit with a stunning strike in the 70th, Ellis Simms restored the two-goal advantage nine minutes from time.

Stephen Mfuni, the 18-year-old center half on loan from Manchester City, scored his first professional goal in the 86th to cap a dream night for Coventry and lift Frank Lampard's men into 14th place.

Newcastle sat three places and two points above them.

Headline: Australia strike twice as South Africa cling on in tense last-day chase
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Durban, AP
Body:
Australia snagged two South Africa wickets in its pursuit of six to win the first cricket Test at Kingsmead on the last day Tuesday.

South Africa restarted on 102-4 with a daunting victory target of 421, and was intent in the morning more on surviving than going for a world record fourth-innings run chase.

The Proteas added only 64 runs in 32 overs to lunch but overnight batters Aiden Markram and Wiaan Mulder comfortably handled the Australia attack on a placid pitch for nearly two hours in the extended session until Josh Hazlewood broke through.

Hazlewood strangled Mulder down the leg side and wicketkeeper Alex Carey took a low one-handed catch.

The end of Mulder's 110-ball innings for 29, after resuming on 5, brought in Kyle Verreynne, who was dismissed on 1 four balls later.

Verreynne tried to cut part-time spinner Matt Renshaw and ended up giving Steve Smith a slips catch and Renshaw his second wicket of the innings, and of his career.

At lunch, South Africa was 166-6 with its hopes pinned on Markram, who was 69 not out from 200 balls, with Marco Jansen on 7.

Headline: Shubman Gill, Shafali Verma ICC players of month
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Dubai, PTI
Body:
India captain Shubman Gill was on Tuesday named ICC men's Player of the Month for a record fifth time following his outstanding performance in the ODI series against the West Indies, while opener Shafali Verma claimed the women's honour for her impressive show in the Asia Cup and the Asian Games.

This is the fifth time players from the same national board have swept the men's and women's awards in a month, with Indian cricketers achieving the feat on two previous occasions.

Jasprit Bumrah and Smriti Mandhana won the honours in June 2024. Abhishek Sharma and Mandhana were the picks for September 2025.

The short-lists for Tuesday's awards featured a host of standout performers, with Abhishek and Zimbabwe captain Sikandar Raza in contention for the men's award.

Sri Lanka captain Chamari Athapaththu and India left-arm spinner Sree Charani were in the reckoning for the women's honour, the ICC said.

Headline: Barcelona, Real Madrid soccer legends to face-off in Kolkata on November 25
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Press Trust of India, Kolkata
Body:
Kolkata is set to host an exhibition match between the legends of Spanish football teams Barcelona and Real Madrid on November 25, West Bengal sports minister Indranil Khan announced on Tuesday.

The match will be held at the Vivekananda Yuva Bharati Krirangan, popularly as the Salt Lake Stadium.

Khan announced the event through a post on his official X handle, hinting at the upcoming fixture between former stars of the two fierce rivals with the message: "El Clasico Loading. #Barcelona Legends vs #RealMadrid Legends. November 25, 2026 Vivekananda Yuva Bharati Krirangan, #Kolkata"

The announcement comes after the iconic venue hosted two high-profile international friendlies featuring the Indian men's football team earlier this month.

India took on five-time world champions Brazil on October 3 before facing two-time world champions Uruguay on October 8, with both matches drawing attention to the city's footballing heritage.

The proposed legends' clash is expected to bring together former players associated with the two Spanish clubs, whose rivalry is among the most celebrated in world football.

Headline: Al-Nassr match in AFC Champions League delayed
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Riyadh, AP
Body:
Cristiano Ronaldo and Al-Nassr's AFC Champions League Elite match against Uzbekistan's Neftchi Fergana was delayed by an hour Monday in Riyadh after Saudi Arabia's Civil Defense issued a security alert.

A sparse crowd at Al-Awwal Park remained in their seats as the teams waited to kick off.

The Saudi-led coalition said it had twice intercepted and destroyed ballistic missiles launched Monday by the Houthi rebels toward Riyadh. At least three explosions could be heard Monday night in the city.

Saudi Arabia's Civil Defense, which had warned residents through alerts sent to their phones, later said the danger had passed.

Al-Nassr won the game 3-1. Angelo Gabriel put the home side ahead in the 33rd minute, and Ronaldo made it 2-0 three minutes before the break. Alisher Odilov pulled a goal back for the visitors before a 78th minute own goal sealed the Saudi team's win.

The Iran-backed Houthis have claimed a series of recent missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia, including strikes on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport that killed at least 13 people and wounded over 300 over the weekend.

Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Associated Press, Madrid
Body:
Under-pressure Manchester United travels to Atletico Madrid in the Champions League on Tuesday, while Barcelona also faces a testing trip to Galatasaray.

United has won just once in the Premier League this season and already exited the English League Cup, turning the spotlight on coach Michael Carrick. A loss to Diego Simeone's Atletico would put Carrick under more scrutiny.

"I understand when it's not going too well there's a real kind of clamour and a real feeling of, it's a disaster, it's all wrong and everything needs to be changed," Carrick said ahead of the match at the Metropolitano. "I kind of get that but that's not in our mindset. That's certainly not in my mindset.

"To achieve anything, really, you've got to be able to deal with things that are difficult and you've got to overcome things. That's just part of it."

There are no such concerns at Barcelona, which has made a 100% winning start to the campaign.

Hansi Flick's team is in Turkey to face Galatasaray and the coach faced questions about whether Barcelona could end its barren run in the Champions League, which it has not won since 2015.

"We have that hunger, and I share it with the team. It's not easy and we have to work very hard. That's what we have to do every day," he said.

"It's a very long journey. We have to stay focused and approach every game with our mentality."

Other games on Tuesday include last season's runner-up Arsenal against Lille, Bayern Munich at Viking and Inter Milan hosting Club Brugge.

Headline: Mumbai crush Chandigarh by an innings and 155 runs
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Press Trust of India, Mumbai
Body:
Mumbai crushed Chandigarh by an innings and 155 runs inside three days of their Elite Group D contest here on Tuesday to begin their Ranji Trophy campaign with a thumping victory that also included a bonus point.

After dismissing Chandigarh for a mere 78 on the second day of their season-opener, Mumbai bowlers dominated the visiting side here at the Wankhede Stadium.

Mumbai had taken a huge lead of 351 runs in the first innings and imposed a follow on the travelling side.

Having taken one wicket on the second evening, Mumbai shot Chandigarh out for another low score of 196 in 52.1 overs with Shams Mulani taking 3/69 and Tanush Kotian claiming 3/30.

Earlier, Mumbai had scored a huge 429 in their first innings with Musheer Khan contributing 65, and captain Siddhesh Lad making a solid 86. The hosts were also further bolstered by half-centuries from Kotian (58) and Mohit Awasthi (56 not out).

Brief scores: Mumbai 429 beat Chandigarh 78 & 196 in 52.1 overs (Shivam Bhambri 44; Tushar Deshpande 2/37, Shams Mulani 3/69, Tanush Kotian 3/30) by an innings and 155 runs.

(Photo caption, as readable: From left, Mumbai's Sylvester D'souza, Mohit Avasthi, Tanush Kotian, Tushar Deshpande and Shams Mulani pose for photograph after winning the Ranji Trophy cricket match between Mumbai and Chandigarh, in Mumbai, Tuesday. PTI)

Ayush Mhatre injured

Mumbai batter Ayush Mhatre has suffered a grade 4 tear in his right hamstring and is facing a lengthy spell on the sidelines, dealing a setback to the side at the start of the Ranji Trophy season.

Mhatre is expected to be out of action for four to six weeks, although a grade 4 hamstring tear can typically require a minimum of three months for recovery, depending on the severity of the injury and the rehabilitation process.

The 19-year-old had spent the last three months at the BCCI's Centre of Excellence (CoE) in Bengaluru, undergoing treatment and rehabilitation for an injury.

The fresh injury to his right hamstring is a major setback for the youngster, who had been named Mumbai's vice-captain for this season's Ranji Trophy.

Mhatre, who has emerged as one of Mumbai's promising young batting talents, will now have to focus on recovery before returning to competitive cricket.

Headline: Thyagarajan, Siraj shine as Hyderabad drub Tripura
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Secunderabad, PTI
Body:
Hyderabad started their Ranji Trophy 2026-27 campaign with an innings and 40-run win over Tripura on the third day of their Elite Group B match here on Tuesday.

Pacers Tanay Thyagarajan and Mohammad Siraj shared eight wickets between them as Hyderabad bundled out Tripura, overnight 99 for seven, for 120 in their second innings.

Thyagarajan was the destroyer-in-chief with a six-wicket haul (6/52) while India pacer Siraj ended up with two for 19 from nine overs.

Thyagarajan dismissed overnight batter Sentu Sarkar early in the first session and Tripura offered little fight thereafter.

Hyderabad amassed seven points from the match, including a bonus point.

Hyderabad will now face Andhra in the second match of the group from October 18, while Tripura will be up against Assam.

Headline: F1 announces return to Africa with grand prix in Rwanda
Section: NT SPORT
Byline: Kigali (Rwanda), AP
Body:
Formula 1 announced on Tuesday that Rwanda will host a race from the early 2030s when the series returns to Africa after about 40 years.

The African Grand Prix will have a 10-year contract and take place at a track in Bugesera, south-east of the capital Kigali. F1 said the purpose-built track will be ready "early in the next decade."

Rwandan President Paul Kagame said at a launch event in Kigali that it was a success and a milestone for the entire continent, especially for the African fans, adding he hoped it could lead to more F1 events in Africa.
