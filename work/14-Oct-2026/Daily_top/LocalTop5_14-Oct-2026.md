NITK Surathkal has operationalised NITKere, a 30-million-litre rainwater harvesting reservoir created in 2025 and linked by an 850-metre pipeline to a one-million-litre-per-day purification plant commissioned in February 2026, presented as a model for institutional water security during summer shortages.

### 5. Bhatkal faces rainfall deficit, inflow into dam declines
Bhatkal in Uttara Kannada is seeing a sharply deficient monsoon versus its normal annual rainfall of 4,152.3 mm, with falling inflow into the Kadavinakatte dam raising concerns for drinking water, about 2,000–2,500 hectares of paddy, and livestock fodder.

### 4. Court issues gag order on airing videos on Siddaramaiah

### 5. KPSC probe: SC nod to replace SIT chief
