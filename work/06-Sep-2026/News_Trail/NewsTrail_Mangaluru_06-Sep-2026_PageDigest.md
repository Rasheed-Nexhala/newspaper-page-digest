### Shakti scheme: Free smart cards for 1 cr women soon

Transport Minister Byrathi Suresh said on Friday that free smart cards will soon be issued to more than one crore women bus passengers under the Shakti scheme for Rs 250 crore, as the first phase of a project that will eventually cover more than three crore women. India's 1976 Semiconductor Complex Limited in Mohali was manufacturing 5,000-nm chips by 1984 before a 1989 fire and bureaucratic delays stalled momentum, making today's multi-billion-dollar subsidies the price of lost silicon sovereignty.
