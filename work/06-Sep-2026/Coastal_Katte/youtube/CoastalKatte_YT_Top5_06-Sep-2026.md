### NUMBER 1 — 'Congress will form Mangaluru railway division if voted to power at Centre'

Number one. Member of Legislative Council P.V. Mohan welcomed the Union government's decision to integrate the Mangaluru railway region with the South Western Railway, but said it should not have been annexed to SWR's Mysuru Division.

He reiterated the long-standing demand for a separate railway division for Mangaluru. He said the Railways generates nearly ₹1,100 crore revenue from coastal Karnataka, and that denial of a division would be a poll issue.

He pledged that Congress would create a separate Mangaluru division if voted to power at the Centre. Dakshina Kannada District Congress President K. Harish Kumar was among those present.

**Host take**

If you live on the coast and feel railway decisions always come from Mysuru or Palakkad, this is the political line you'll hear through the next election cycle.

### NUMBER 2 — Vande Bharat runtime trial held between Yeshwantpur, Mangaluru

Number two. The South Western Railway conducted a runtime trial of a 16-car Vande Bharat Express rake between Yeshwantpur and Mangaluru Central on Saturday, September 5, ahead of a proposed regular service linking Bengaluru and coastal Karnataka.

The 2023-make rake, fitted with automatic emergency braking, became the first train-set to run on electric traction end-to-end on this sector.

But it reached Mangaluru at 4.25 p.m. instead of 2.25 p.m. after technical glitches caused by unstable overhead electric equipment on the Sakleshpur–Subrahmanya Road Ghat section.

Divisional Railway Manager Mudit Mittal said further trials may be held before finalising the timetable, and that the proposed running time was higher than comparable services.

**Host take**

A faster Bengaluru–Mangaluru link is on the table — but this trial shows the ghat section still needs work before anyone can trust the clock.

### NUMBER 3 — Expired food products seized

Number three. Food safety officials, acting on a complaint submitted via a Karnataka Health and Welfare Department QR code, seized expired food items from Reliance Smart Bazaar in Attavara on Saturday.

A Kodialbail resident found parota with a manufactured date of June 1 and expiry date of June 7 ordered through the Jio Mart App.

Additional samples were seized and sent for laboratory examination, and a show-cause notice was issued to the hypermarket management.

**Host take**

If you order groceries through an app in Mangaluru, check the dates when the packet arrives — this case started with one customer complaint through a QR code.

### NUMBER 4 — Cops assaulted while attending to domestic violence plaint

Number four. Two Puttur Rural Police Station personnel — Head Constable Madhu and Constable Bheemana Gowda M Biradar — were allegedly assaulted and abused by a man and his family while responding to a domestic violence complaint at Mundaje in Padnur village on Friday night.

When the police intervened after the woman's husband Naveen attempted to assault her again, Naveen, his mother and brother allegedly obstructed the officers.

Naveen is undergoing hospital treatment and further legal action will be taken after discharge.

**Host take**

This is about what happens when officers respond to a domestic violence call in a coastal village — and whether the law can protect the protectors too.

### NUMBER 5 — 'Department of Praja Seva meant to hear and redress grievances of citizens'

Number five. Dakshina Kannada District in-charge Minister U.T. Khader inaugurated the district-level Praja Seva meeting in Puttur on Saturday, saying the government has established the Department of Praja Seva to hear and redress the grievances of every citizen and deliver the benefits of every government scheme.

On the inaugural day, a total of 163 applications were received from the public, including 61 for Revenue, 22 for Rural Development and 13 for Police.

Complaints covered land sanction, plotting, title deeds, survey, gun licences, bus sanctions and provision of teachers for schools.

Mr. Khader distributed title deeds to beneficiaries and said issues pending for 20 to 30 years have been resolved. Puttur MLA Ashok Kumar Rai and district-level officials were present.

**Host take**

If you have a land or revenue file stuck for years, this is the kind of doorstep camp the state says it will run — 163 people showed up on day one in Puttur alone.
