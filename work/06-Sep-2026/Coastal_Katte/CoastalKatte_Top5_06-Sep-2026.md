### 1. ‘Congress will form Mangaluru railway division if voted to power at Centre’

Member of Legislative Council P.V. Mohan welcomed the Union government’s decision to integrate the Mangaluru railway region with the South Western Railway but said it should not have been annexed to SWR’s Mysuru Division, reiterating the long-standing demand for a separate railway division for Mangaluru. ### 2. Vande Bharat runtime trial held between Yeshwantpur, Mangaluru

The South Western Railway conducted a runtime trial of a 16-car Vande Bharat Express rake between Yeshwantpur and Mangaluru Central on Saturday, September 5, ahead of a proposed regular service linking Bengaluru and coastal Karnataka. The 2023-make rake, fitted with automatic emergency braking, became the first train-set to run on electric traction end-to-end on the sector, though it reached Mangaluru at 4.25 p.m. instead of 2.25 p.m. after technical glitches caused by unstable overhead electric equipment on the Sakleshpur–Subrahmanya Road Ghat section; Divisional Railway Manager Mudit Mittal said further trials may be held before finalising the timetable and that the proposed running time was higher than comparable services.

### 5. 'Department of Praja Seva meant to hear and redress grievances of citizens'

Dakshina Kannada District in-charge Minister U.T. Khader inaugurated the district-level Praja Seva meeting in Puttur on Saturday, saying the government has established the Department of Praja Seva to hear and redress the grievances of every citizen and deliver the benefits of every government scheme. On the inaugural day, a total of 163 applications were received from the public, including 61 for Revenue, 22 for Rural Development and 13 for Police; complaints covered land sanction, plotting, title deeds, survey, gun licences, bus sanctions and provision of teachers for schools. Mr. Khader distributed title deeds to beneficiaries and said issues pending for 20 to 30 years have been resolved; Puttur MLA Ashok Kumar Rai and district-level officials were present.
