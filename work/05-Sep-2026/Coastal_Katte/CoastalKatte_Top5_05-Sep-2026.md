### 2. Worm allegedly found inside egg: Blinkit facility sealed
Food safety officials inspected a Blinkit online retail facility in Mangaluru after a customer alleged that a worm was found inside an egg purchased through the quick-commerce platform, with the complaint reportedly submitted to Health Minister U T Khader. During inspection of the facility at Falnir Square in Falnir, officials found packets containing broken eggs and directed staff to remove and dispose of affected stock; the storage room was temporarily closed and sealed for cleaning and sanitisation, and the Food Safety Department said it would issue a notice seeking an explanation. In a separate action in Bengaluru, the Department of Food Safety and Drug Administration closed and seized a kitchen at Chairman's Club in Sahakarnagar's Dollars Colony after finding around 40 kg of expired chilli and alleged violations including improper labelling, unhygienic conditions and storage of vegetarian and non-vegetarian food together.

### 3. 16-coach Vande Bharat to undergo Yeshwanthpur–Mangaluru test run
A 16-coach Vande Bharat Express will undertake a trial run between Yeshwanthpur and Mangaluru Central on Saturday, September 5, as directed by the Railway Board. The train will leave Yeshwanthpur at 6.05am and arrive at Mangaluru Central at 2.25pm, halting at Hassan, Sakleshpur, Subrahmanya Road and Padil; it returns from Mangaluru Central at 3pm and reaches Yeshwanthpur at 11pm. The trial assumes importance as the route includes the challenging ghat section, where officials will assess operational performance under varying gradients and explore reducing the proposed journey time of more than eight hours without compromising safety.

### 4. No end to cyber fraud: ₹26 crore lost in two and a half years
Despite sustained awareness campaigns, cyber fraud continues to pose a serious threat in Uttara Kannada, with fraudsters allegedly siphoning off nearly ₹26 crore from residents over the past two and a half years. According to the state Home Department, 237 cyber fraud cases were registered at the Uttara Kannada cyber police station during the period, 14 accused were arrested, and only around ₹1.85 crore could be recovered; fraudsters have used fake online job offers, stock market investment scams, impersonation of bank or government officials, KYC update requests and social media targeting. The Uttara Kannada police have announced a video competition on cyber safety, with submissions due to the district police social media cell by September 20.
