### NUMBER 1 — Two NIT-K professors held over fake bills

Number one. Police arrested two NIT-K Surathkal associate NCC officers — P. Sam Johnson of the Department of Mathematical and Computational Sciences and H. Shivananda Nayaka of the Department of Mechanical Engineering — for claiming ₹13.51 lakh in travel, accommodation and daily allowance using forged NCC camp documents.

Director B. Ravi's complaint alleged Johnson swindled ₹12.32 lakh and Nayaka ₹1.18 lakh for duties claimed between May 5, 2023 and May 16, 2026, including ₹1.18 lakh each for a Pre-Thal Sainik Camp-II in Bengaluru from June 28 to July 7, 2025 using the same fake detailment order.

**Host take**

NIT-K Surathkal is one of the biggest institutions on this coast — and this is about public money claimed through NCC duty bills that police say were backed by forged papers.

### NUMBER 2 — Worm allegedly found inside egg: Blinkit facility sealed

Number two. Food safety officials inspected a Blinkit online retail facility in Mangaluru after a customer alleged that a worm was found inside an egg purchased through the quick-commerce platform, with the complaint reportedly submitted to Health Minister U T Khader.

During inspection of the facility at Falnir Square in Falnir, officials found packets containing broken eggs and directed staff to remove and dispose of affected stock. The storage room was temporarily closed and sealed for cleaning and sanitisation, and the Food Safety Department said it would issue a notice seeking an explanation.

**Host take**

If you order groceries through quick-commerce apps in Mangaluru, this is the kind of complaint that triggered a full inspection and a sealed storage room — not just a refund chat.

### NUMBER 3 — 16-coach Vande Bharat to undergo Yeshwanthpur–Mangaluru test run

Number three. A 16-coach Vande Bharat Express will undertake a trial run between Yeshwanthpur and Mangaluru Central on Saturday, September 5, as directed by the Railway Board.

The train will leave Yeshwanthpur at 6.05am and arrive at Mangaluru Central at 2.25pm, halting at Hassan, Sakleshpur, Subrahmanya Road and Padil. It returns from Mangaluru Central at 3pm and reaches Yeshwanthpur at 11pm.

The trial assumes importance as the route includes the challenging ghat section, where officials will assess operational performance under varying gradients and explore reducing the proposed journey time of more than eight hours without compromising safety.

**Host take**

This is a real test on the ghat section — not just a timetable announcement — and it could shape how fast Bengaluru and Mangaluru get linked by Vande Bharat if the run goes well.

### NUMBER 4 — No end to cyber fraud: ₹26 crore lost in two and a half years

Number four. Despite sustained awareness campaigns, cyber fraud continues to pose a serious threat in Uttara Kannada, with fraudsters allegedly siphoning off nearly ₹26 crore from residents over the past two and a half years.

According to the state Home Department, 237 cyber fraud cases were registered at the Uttara Kannada cyber police station during the period, 14 accused were arrested, and only around ₹1.85 crore could be recovered. Fraudsters have used fake online job offers, stock market investment scams, impersonation of bank or government officials, KYC update requests and social media targeting.

The Uttara Kannada police have announced a video competition on cyber safety, with submissions due to the district police social media cell by September 20.

**Host take**

₹26 crore lost and only about ₹1.85 crore recovered — if you have family on the coast who get random KYC or investment messages, this is the scale police are dealing with right now.

### NUMBER 5 — Traffic advisory issued ahead of Mosaru Kudike celebrations

Number five. The Mangaluru City Police on Friday issued a traffic advisory for Mosaru Kudike programmes across the city from Saturday evening on Sri Krishna Janmashtami, urging motorists to avoid congested routes.

Commissioner of Police Sudheer Kumar Reddy cited last year's traffic snarls and advised alternative routes for Thokkottu-Olapete celebrations on NH-66 via Konaje Main Road and Deralakatte for Talapady-bound traffic, Kadri Sri Gopalakrishna Mutt via Bejai-Pinto Bakery-Karangalpady or Mallikatte-Nantoor, Attavara Main Road via Valencia or Ananda Shetty Circle-S.L. Mathias Road-Highland Junction, and Kulur Ferry Road near Kottara via KSRTC-Kuntikana.

**Host take**

Janmashtami evening in Mangaluru means Mosaru Kudike crowds — save this advisory before you pick a route on Saturday.
