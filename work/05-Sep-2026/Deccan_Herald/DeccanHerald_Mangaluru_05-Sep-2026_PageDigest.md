### Tunnel may damage Lalbagh rock, warns GSI

A Geological Survey of India expert committee has flagged serious geological, structural and groundwater risks to Lalbagh and its three-billion-year-old Lalbagh rock in a 27-page report submitted to the government in April. The committee warned that tunneling and excavation could damage the ancient rock mass, widen existing cracks and threaten the Kempegowda tower, and flagged proposed Shaft 4 adjacent to the National Geological Monument as a critical concern. ### Urine causes melting of Everest Base Camp: Study

A new study estimates about 3 million kilograms of glacial ice and snow are lost each spring at Everest Base Camp from energy used for climber comforts, with human urine alone responsible for an estimated 154 tons of melt on the Khumbu Glacier. In 2023, researchers recorded 824 cylinders of liquefied petroleum gas, 18,935 litres of kerosene and 5,130 litres of gasoline used for cooking, heating and powering about 1,600 tents, while more than 6,000 litres of urine are produced daily by climbers and guides, most of it deposited directly on the glacier rather than in pit-vault toilets.

### Air pollution, biofilms and the lung

Bengaluru's worsening air quality in late 2025, with AQI levels around 150–180, joins Delhi research led by JNU's Professor Kasturi Mukhopadhyay finding antibiotic-resistant bacteria in polluted air, raising concern that PM2.5 may carry resistant microbes into lungs and worsen COPD affecting over 55 million Indians, many non-smokers. Bengaluru's residential real estate market is entering a more mature phase in 2026, with healthy demand but increasingly selective buyers weighing connectivity, open spaces, larger homes, amenities and developer credibility alongside Namma Metro expansion and road improvements that are opening peripheral pockets beyond traditional hubs.
