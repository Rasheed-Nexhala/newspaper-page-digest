## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. MRPL blast: Another victim succumbs to injuries at hospital
Rajesh D'Silva, in his 40s, died on Saturday from injuries sustained in the 30 September explosion at MRPL’s Coker Hydrotreater unit, raising the toll to two after Manish Karkada of Mulky died on the day of the accident; MRPL expressed condolences and announced ₹90 lakh compensation and other deliverables, totalling ₹1.20 crore for the family.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.6; News Trail Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Praja Seva aims to take govt. closer to the people: Khader
Dakshina Kannada district in-charge Minister U.T. Khader launched the Praja Seva initiative at Mangalore University campus, Konaje, to streamline administration and ensure welfare schemes reach eligible beneficiaries; he received 209 petitions at the event. The Hindu Bureau reports from Mangaluru.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Konkan Railway to switch to non-monsoon schedule from Wednesday across its network
Konkan Railway Corporation Ltd will revert to regular non-monsoon timings from Wednesday, October 21, with revised arrival and departure times for trains including Matsyagandha Express and Vande Bharat Express across its network. The Hindu Bureau reports from Mangaluru.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 4. Pilinalike-11 to be held in Mangaluru on October 20; winner to get ₹10 lakh
The 11th Pilinalike tiger-dance competition organised by the Pilinalike Foundation and Namma TV will be held at Karavali Utsav Grounds near Mangala Stadium from 10 am on 20 October, with nine teams expected; the winner gets ₹10 lakh and a trophy, second ₹5 lakh, third ₹3 lakh, category prizes of ₹50,000 each, ₹50,000 per team incentive, and Dinesh Karthik and The Great Khali among expected guests.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5; News Trail Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 5. Special trains to clear extra rush during festive season
Southern Railway will run Train No. 06036 Chennai Egmore–Mangaluru Central leaving 11.55 pm on 17 October and arriving 7.45 pm on 18 October, and Train No. 06142 Mangaluru Central–Rameswaram leaving 4.15 am on 19 October and reaching Rameswaram 7.30 pm the same day, with listed halts en route.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Old kitchens await repairs, renovations in schools
Naina J A reports many Dakshina Kannada school kitchens serving Akshara Dasoha midday meals to 1.37 lakh students in 1,312 schools are dilapidated or lack space, while workers' association leaders demand renovation grants stalled since 2012 and timely honorarium payments.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5_

### 2. MLA raises objections to moving old beds, cots to new district hospital
Udupi MLA Yashpal Suvarna criticised the government's plan to shift old equipment from Udupi Taluk Hospital to the new district hospital building without adequate new infrastructure or sufficient doctors and technicians. The Hindu Bureau reports from Mangaluru.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Coastal K'taka needs High Court Bench, say advocates
The Coastal Karnataka High Court Bench Struggle Committee met at Brahmavara Bunts Bhavan urging the Chief Justice and governments to establish a circuit bench, with advocates citing 20–30% of coastal cases in the High Court and nearly 500 km travel to Bengaluru; speakers including Prasad Raj Kanchan and Kadooru Praveen Kumar Shetty sought a bench convenient to Dakshina Kannada, Udupi and Uttara Kannada.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 4. Panaje records 167 mm rainfall
Heavy rain lashed Dakshina Kannada with Panaje recording 167 mm; Belthangady and Mangaluru also saw significant rainfall as IMD forecast more showers for three days from a cyclonic circulation over the Arabian Sea.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5_

### 5. Three booked for knife-point robbery of bikers in Belthangady
Police booked three men for allegedly intercepting a motorcycle near Haleji in Uruvalu, Belthangady, around 11.45 am on 6 October, threatening Mithilesh, 18, and Magadevi with a knife and taking phones worth ₹3,000 and ₹2,000 and ₹5,200 cash.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 1. Drought grips entire state; gloomy forecast spells doomsday
Sujay B M reports 226 of 240 taluks are drought-affected with estimated crop loss exceeding ₹60,000 crore, surpassing the ₹35,162 crore loss in 2023-24, as a CM-led team plans to meet the Prime Minister seeking central relief.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 2. Over 1.28 lakh Form 7s filed just before Karnataka SIR; 45,878 by third parties
More than 1.28 lakh Form 7 deletion requests were filed between announcement of the special intensive revision and freezing of rolls in at least 28 Assembly constituencies, with third-party applications making up 35.6% of deletions; Belagavi North saw 4,750 processed Form 7s. Rishika Khanna and P.S. Vasanth B.A. report from Bengaluru and Chennai.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

### 3. State plans standardisation of private hospital charges
The Karnataka Health Department is considering uniform pricing for surgeries and procedures at private hospitals after preliminary talks with PHANA, as patients in Bengaluru, Hubballi and Mangaluru report the same treatment costing from about ₹10,000 at one facility to ₹50,000 or up to ₹1 lakh at corporate hospitals; officials may decide on a mechanism as early as next week.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.2_

### 4. Chief Minister announces funds for Mekedatu project in next Budget
Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar said funds for the Mekedatu balancing reservoir project will be allocated in the next State budget. The Hindu Bureau reports from Mysuru.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 5. Stir against SIR; Over 45 detained
Police detained over 45 AISA protesters at Bengaluru Town Hall during a stir against SIR and alleged police brutality in Delhi; a few suffered bruises as officers dragged them away after denying permission citing a High Court order restricting protests to Freedom Park.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

## Top 5 — India

### 1. 17 killed, 25 injured in Kolkata puja stampede
A stampede at the Chore Bagan Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata on Saturday left 17 people dead and 25 injured; West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee blamed a misleading announcement by organisers for the crush. Shrabana Chatterjee and Shiv Sahay Singh report from Kolkata.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Relief to India as Trump waives Russia sanctions
New Delhi expects to continue importing Russian crude after President Trump waived sanctions under the Lindsey Graham Sanctioning Russia Act, with FICCI and industry voices citing relief for refiners and energy security.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 3. Govt. caps trade margins on 137 drugs against cancer
The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority capped trade margins at 30% on 137 non-scheduled cancer drug formulations.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_

### 4. Children have fundamental right to access Internet: SC
The Supreme Court held that children have a fundamental right to access the internet and that blanket bans are disproportionate, calling for parental oversight, age-appropriate safeguards and a dedicated child mode from tech stakeholders. Krishnadas Rajagopal reports from New Delhi.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_

### 5. 5 killed in blast at chemical unit in Gujarat's Bharuch
Five people died and several were injured in a blast at Syndoxal Pharma intermediates unit in the Saykha GIDC area of Bharuch district, Gujarat.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_
