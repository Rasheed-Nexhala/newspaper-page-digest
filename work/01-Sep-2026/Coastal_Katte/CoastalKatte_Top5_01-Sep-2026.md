### 1. Auto fares hiked in Mangaluru from today

Autorickshaw fares will go up in Mangaluru on Tuesday, with the minimum for the first 1.5 km rising from ₹35 to ₹40 and every subsequent kilometre from ₹15 to ₹20, after the RTA headed by Dakshina Kannada Deputy Commissioner H.V. Darshan approved the revision in an order on Friday citing rising costs of fuel, CNG, and spare parts. There will be no waiting charge for the first 10 minutes and ₹5 for every subsequent 15 minutes; between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. drivers may collect 1.5 times the normal fare, and the revised chart must be displayed with meters recalibrated. Dakshina Kannada is now on a par with Udupi and Shivamogga (₹40 and ₹20 from September 14, 2022 and November 14, 2022); previous DK fares of ₹35 and ₹17 per subsequent km had been in effect since October 27, 2022.

### 2. New MUDA chairman to get State nod for draft Master Plan III on priority

Newly nominated MUDA chairman Santhosh Kumar Shetty said on Monday his first priority was getting government approval for the draft Master Plan III for the Mangaluru Local Planning Area, submitted in January and awaiting nod for over seven months; Master Plan II came into effect October 1, 2009, and Plan III should have been in place by 2019. He will discuss it with Urban Development Minister Yatindra Siddaramaiah and District In-charge Minister U.T. Khader; once approved, public objections will be invited, and he said MUDA would take up infrastructure beyond MCC jurisdiction in areas such as Surathkal and Bajpe, explore funding for an underpass or flyover, and consider buying private land for parks. Of 200 housing sites on 18 acres at Kunjathbail, 35 have been distributed; 142 sites on 13.5 acres at Konaje are yet to be distributed; work has begun on 700 sites on 45 acres at Chelairu.

### 3. MCC body makes 4-way waste segregation a must

The Mangaluru City Corporation has made segregation of waste into wet, dry, sanitary and special-care categories mandatory at source for residents and all bulk waste generators, giving citizens one month to comply before collection staff refuse improperly segregated waste. Wet and sanitary waste will be collected daily, dry and special-care waste on Fridays, and violations will attract penalties under the MCC Solid Waste Management Bye-laws, 2019.

### 4. Puttur Cong MLA writes to CM, alleges corruption in KRIDL

Puttur MLA Ashok Kumar Rai has urged Chief Minister D K Shivakumar to order an investigation into allegations of corruption, illegal collection of money and threats to contractors by officials of the Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited (KRIDL), Mangaluru division, in a letter dated August 25. Rai alleged KRIDL Mangaluru Executive Engineer Vijay and Junior Engineer Prajwal collected additional amounts of up to seven per cent from contractors in the name of a "KRIDL charge" and that contractors were told monthly payments had to be made to the district in-charge and department ministers. Leader of Opposition R Ashoka said on X that the ruling Congress government's corruption had been exposed by one of its own legislators.

### 5. Food safety crackdown on 16 sweet manufacturing units in state; major violations found

Food Safety and Drug Administration officials on Monday conducted a special inspection drive at sweet manufacturing units in Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural, Greater Bengaluru Authority, Mysuru, Dakshina Kannada, Belagavi, Kalaburagi and Hubballi-Dharwad, collecting samples of sweets, cooking oil, khova, Mysore Pak and other products for laboratory examination. Major violations included storage of expired food products, labelling not in accordance with FSSAI regulations, use of unauthorised artificial colours and failure to mention mandatory label declarations; units inspected included Asha Sweets and Bikaner Sweets in Bengaluru, Swagath Sweets in Mangaluru and World of Mysore Pak in Mysuru. Officials also found incorrect labelling, improper storage, unhygienic manufacturing conditions and other violations during the drive.
