## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. New MUDA chairman to get State nod for draft Master Plan III on priority
Newly nominated MUDA chairman Santhosh Kumar Shetty said on Monday his first priority was getting government approval for the draft Master Plan III for the Mangaluru Local Planning Area, submitted in January and awaiting nod for over seven months; Master Plan II came into effect October 1, 2009, and Plan III should have been in place by 2019. He will discuss it with Urban Development Minister Yatindra Siddaramaiah and District In-charge Minister U.T. Khader; once approved, public objections will be invited, and he said MUDA would take up infrastructure beyond MCC jurisdiction in areas such as Surathkal and Bajpe, explore funding for an underpass or flyover, and consider buying private land for parks. Of 200 housing sites on 18 acres at Kunjathbail, 35 have been distributed; 142 sites on 13.5 acres at Konaje are yet to be distributed; work has begun on 700 sites on 45 acres at Chelairu.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 2. MCC body makes 4-way waste segregation a must
The Mangaluru City Corporation has made segregation of waste into wet, dry, sanitary and special-care categories mandatory at source for residents and all bulk waste generators, giving citizens one month to comply before collection staff refuse improperly segregated waste. Wet and sanitary waste will be collected daily, dry and special-care waste on Fridays, and violations will attract penalties under the MCC Solid Waste Management Bye-laws, 2019.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Cabinet meeting in Mangaluru on Sept 18
The Karnataka government has decided to hold a State Cabinet meeting in Mangaluru on September 18, with proposals concerning the coastal districts likely to receive special priority. Chief Secretary Shalini Rajneesh has directed Additional Chief Secretaries, Principal Secretaries and Secretaries of all government departments to immediately prepare coastal-district proposals and place them before the Cabinet in the form of Cabinet notes.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; News Trail Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 4. Auto fares hiked in Mangaluru from today
Autorickshaw fares will go up in Mangaluru on Tuesday, with the minimum for the first 1.5 km rising from ₹35 to ₹40 and every subsequent kilometre from ₹15 to ₹20, after the RTA headed by Dakshina Kannada Deputy Commissioner H.V. Darshan approved the revision in an order on Friday citing rising costs of fuel, CNG, and spare parts. There will be no waiting charge for the first 10 minutes and ₹5 for every subsequent 15 minutes; between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. drivers may collect 1.5 times the normal fare, and the revised chart must be displayed with meters recalibrated. Dakshina Kannada is now on a par with Udupi and Shivamogga (₹40 and ₹20 from September 14, 2022 and November 14, 2022); previous DK fares of ₹35 and ₹17 per subsequent km had been in effect since October 27, 2022.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 5. Chetan R assumes charge as new DIGP Western Range
Chetan R assumed charge as the new Deputy Inspector General of Police (DIGP), Western Range, on Monday morning from outgoing IGP (western range) Amith Singh.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Hindu organisations seek arrest of group that assaulted photocopy centre owners
Amid heavy police presence, a large number of people joined the ‘Puttur Chalo Rally’ on Monday, jointly organised by the VHP, Bajrang Dal and Hindu Jagarana Vedike, demanding arrest of those who allegedly attacked copying-centre owners in Puttur on August 21. HJV member Shrikant Shetty Karkala said a complaint accusing the owners of assault was registered for attempt to murder, but no serious charges were filed against those who assaulted Sudharshan Pai and relatives Akshata Pai and Sadashiva Pai in the presence of police. The rally started around 11 a.m., led by Sullia MLA Bhagirathi Murulya, with Bantwal MLA Rajesh Naik, Mangaluru City North MLA Y. Bharath Shetty, MLC Kishore Kumar Puttur and others taking part, and ended around 12.30 p.m. at Venkataramana temple.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 2. Class 9 student ends life for scoring less marks in exam
Shravan (14) of Kokkada allegedly died by suicide on Sunday after being upset over scoring three out of 40 marks in Hindi, his uncle Sathish Gowda K told the Dharmasthala Police in a complaint filed on Monday. Shravan's father had attended a parent-teacher meeting at the private school on Saturday, where a teacher informed him of the low score; the boy reportedly locked himself in his study room and was found dead later. The Dharmasthala Police have registered a case.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4_

### 3. Puttur Cong MLA writes to CM, alleges corruption in KRIDL
Puttur MLA Ashok Kumar Rai has urged Chief Minister D K Shivakumar to order an investigation into allegations of corruption, illegal collection of money and threats to contractors by officials of the Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited (KRIDL), Mangaluru division, in a letter dated August 25. Rai alleged KRIDL Mangaluru Executive Engineer Vijay and Junior Engineer Prajwal collected additional amounts of up to seven per cent from contractors in the name of a "KRIDL charge" and that contractors were told monthly payments had to be made to the district in-charge and department ministers. Leader of Opposition R Ashoka said on X that the ruling Congress government's corruption had been exposed by one of its own legislators.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.6; The Hindu Mangaluru p.6_

### 4. Bhatkal hospital to get mother & child care block
Karnataka Health and Family Welfare, Minority Welfare, Haj and Wakf Minister U.T. Khader said a dedicated Mother and Child Care block is proposed at Bhatkal Taluk Hospital to improve healthcare and ease pressure on the existing facility, which has a sanctioned capacity of 100 beds but often admits more than 130 patients. Khader said steps would be taken to establish a separate MCC unit if the hospital records more than 100 deliveries a month, and that a meeting with MLA Mankal Vaidya and senior officials would be held soon to finalise plans; he also said the foundation stone for a Haj Bhavan in Mangaluru would be laid soon.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 5. Puttur temple devotees seek probe into alleged irregularities
Devotees and citizens of Puttur have petitioned the Karnataka Department of Religious Endowments seeking an inquiry into alleged administrative irregularities, rule violations and controversial developmental works at Sri Mahalingeshwara Temple, including demolition of the Naga Sannidhi, Ayyappa Swamy and Navagraha shrines without prior approval. The complaint also questions the dismantling of a Nataraja platform and a 3,000 sq ft building, the cutting of more than 15 coconut trees, filling of at least seven open wells, and a proposed 30-foot road along the northern side that complainants say could use around four acres of temple property.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 1. HC quashes land acquisition for KHB project
The High Court of Karnataka has quashed acquisition of 1,938 acres for the Karnataka Housing Board’s Suryanagar 4th phase residential layout, holding the land is part of areas proposed as eco-sensitive zone of Bannerghatta National Park and abuts elephant corridors; a Division Bench of Justice D.K. Singh and Justice H. Shanthi Bhushan passed the order on August 29, setting aside a single judge’s January 2025 verdict. The Bench said since the project falls within BNP’s proposed ESZ of 268.96 sq. km., the whole project is misconceived and would have irreversible ecological consequences; KHB began acquisition in 2013 across five Anekal taluk villages — Konasandra, Bommandahalli, Kadujakkanahalli, Indlavadi, and Bagganadoddi. MoEFCC proposed an ESZ of 268.96 sq. km in a 2016 draft, later reduced to 168.84 sq. km in a March 2020 final notification; the CEC’s January 2026 report on a plea by K.B. Belliappa recommended restoring 268.96 sq. km, while MoEFCC said it is studying that recommendation.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 2. KPSC scam: CID arrests middleman who made crores
The Criminal Investigation Department has arrested Basavaraj Kannale, a Bhalki-based alleged middleman, in connection with the KPSC veterinary officers recruitment scam, opening a new line of investigation into the suspected network behind alleged recruitment irregularities. Investigators are examining allegations that he used purported political contacts and photographs with leaders of both the BJP and Congress to convince aspirants he had influence over recruitment, and are probing his financial dealings, communications and links with officials including an alleged connection with IAS officer Gyanendra Kumar. He is allegedly the owner of property in Bengaluru and Bhalki and is also alleged to have purchased land worth crores of rupees in his native village.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.6; News Trail Mangaluru p.2; The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 3. Rs 72 lakh for 30x40 site: BDA starts accepting applications for Karanth layout
Chief Minister D K Shivakumar on Monday announced allotment of 2,815 sites to the general public in the 3,700-acre Dr K Shivaram Karanth Layout spread over 17 villages in northern Bengaluru, with a 30x40 sq ft site priced at Rs 72 lakh at Rs 6,000 per square foot. The Bangalore Development Authority (BDA) has started accepting applications through the Seva Sindhu portal; the layout comprises 24,714 sites in total, with 18,399 reserved for landowners who parted with land in 2020-21 and the project costing Rs 16,419.89 crore. Shivakumar hinted it could be the BDA's last layout given land acquisition difficulties and said site prices would rise further when the Bengaluru Business Corridor comes up.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5_

### 4. 13 from K'taka still untraced after floods: CM
Karnataka Chief Minister D K Shivakumar said on Monday that about 13 people from the state were yet to be traced following flash floods along the Nepal-Tibet border and adjoining areas last week. The state government is in contact with the Ministry of External Affairs and the central government, with representatives sent to establish contact; Shivakumar said one group has not been traced while the rest have been found.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.7; News Trail Mangaluru p.1; The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

### 5. Food safety crackdown on 16 sweet manufacturing units in state; major violations found
Food Safety and Drug Administration officials on Monday conducted a special inspection drive at sweet manufacturing units in Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural, Greater Bengaluru Authority, Mysuru, Dakshina Kannada, Belagavi, Kalaburagi and Hubballi-Dharwad, collecting samples of sweets, cooking oil, khova, Mysore Pak and other products for laboratory examination. Major violations included storage of expired food products, labelling not in accordance with FSSAI regulations, use of unauthorised artificial colours and failure to mention mandatory label declarations; units inspected included Asha Sweets and Bikaner Sweets in Bengaluru, Swagath Sweets in Mangaluru and World of Mysore Pak in Mysuru. Officials also found incorrect labelling, improper storage, unhygienic manufacturing conditions and other violations during the drive.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5; The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

## Top 5 — India

### 1. India fastest growing economy with 7.8 pc in Q1
India's economy grew at a faster-than-expected 7.8 per cent in the April–June quarter, slowing from 8.6 per cent in the previous quarter but remaining well above expectations and exceeding the Reserve Bank of India's forecast of 7 per cent. The growth showed resilience despite concerns that the war in Iran and global economic uncertainty could weigh on expansion and disrupt energy markets.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Indus water pact in force, rules Hague court; India rejects
A World Bank-constituted court of arbitration ruled on Monday that the more than six-decade-old Indus Water Treaty between India and Pakistan remains fully in force despite New Delhi putting it in abeyance after the April 22, 2025 Pahalgam terror attack that killed 26 Indian citizens. India rejected the Hague-based award as an illegally constituted body with no jurisdiction over sovereign decisions, while the tribunal unanimously found no grounds for suspension or termination and barred India from concreting the dam wall and power intake of the 850-MW Ralte Hydroelectric Plant on the Chenab beyond certain levels until 90 days after the Neutral Expert's final decision, expected in July 2027.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. 14% monsoon shortfall so far, Sept too will be deficient: IMD
Three months into the southwest monsoon season, all southern states remain severely rain deficient with little hope of recovery in September, which is likely to receive below-normal showers, the India Meteorological Department said on Monday, adding that August was the hottest month on record since 1901. Karnataka has a cumulative shortfall of 23%, Andhra Pradesh tops the list at 42%, Kerala and Tamil Nadu are both at 21%, Telangana at 19%, and the national deficiency is 14%, with September rainfall expected at 91% of the 167.9 mm average.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 4. UP teen gang-raped in running bus in Delhi
A 16-year-old girl was allegedly gang-raped by bus driver Kuldeep (39) and conductor Sandeep (26), both residents of Kanpur, in a private bus that ran unchecked for more than 40 km across Delhi in early August. The class XI student from Uttar Pradesh's Mainpuri had mistakenly got off at Pari Chowk, Greater Noida, on August 4 when the conductor allegedly told her the bus was headed to Noida Sector 63; both accused were arrested hours after a complaint was filed.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.1_

### 5. Over 6 crore names vanish from draft voters' lists in 17 states, UTs
Nearly 6.15 crore names have been deleted from draft electoral rolls in 17 states and Union Territories during the third phase of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), pruning lists from 36.08 crore voters to 29.93 crore—a 17.04% (6,15,07,279) reduction owing to death, permanent shifting, multiple enrolments and other reasons. Delhi recorded the highest proportion of deletions at 32.78% (47.56 lakh), Maharashtra the highest number at 2.06 crore (21.15%), and Karnataka 1.07 crore names omitted (19.47%), with Telangana third at 73.39 lakh.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — International

### 1. Nepal flood toll 900; over 4,200 still missing
The toll in devastating flash floods and mudslides caused by a glacial collapse in Nepal has gone over 900, with more than 4,200 people reported missing, including 590 foreign nationals and 99 security personnel. Triggered by a wave of ice, rocks, and debris along the Bhote Koshi River, the disaster has affected central and northern regions, leaving at least 933 workers trapped across six hydropower projects; Prime Minister Balendra Shah called it an "unimaginable and heartbreaking" calamity.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Trump vows 'hard' response after fresh exchange of fire with Iran
Donald Trump threatened to hit Iran "hard" after the two foes traded fire for the first time in over a month, with the US saying it carried out strikes on Iranian rocket launchers on the island of Larak in the Strait of Hormuz and Iran retaliating with missile and drone attacks aimed at Jordan and the United Arab Emirates. State media reported two people were killed in the US attack on Larak, while President Masoud Pezeshkian later called for dialogue, saying continuing the war was neither in Iran's interest nor in the interest of the region.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12; News Trail Mangaluru p.9_

### 3. Congo Ebola outbreak tops 6,000 cases
Congo's authorities said Monday that the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak in history has topped 6,000 cases, with 2,911 deaths, as the outbreak in eastern Congo spreads under extremely difficult conditions fuelled by insecurity, displacement, a health workers' strike and intense population movements.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_

### 4. Modi meets Iranian Prez, calls for end to war
Prime Minister Narendra Modi told Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on the sidelines of the SCO summit in Bishkek on Monday that India wants to expand and diversify trade with Iran and called for resolution of the West Asia conflict through dialogue and diplomacy. The MEA said Modi expressed concern at escalating tensions, reiterated support for efforts ensuring lasting peace, and emphasised that civilians, civilian infrastructure and commercial shipping must not be harmed.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.9_

### 5. North Korea vows to strengthen nuclear force
North Korea said on Monday the United States' hostile policy remained unchanged and vowed to continue strengthening its nuclear force, rejecting repeated U.S. calls for its denuclearisation. In a statement carried by state media KCNA, a Foreign Ministry spokesperson accused Washington of maintaining a confrontational stance through its commitment to the "complete denuclearisation" of North Korea, criticism of the country's human rights record and planned joint military exercises with South Korea and Japan.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_

## Top 5 — Sports

### 1. Final whistle: Messi calls time on glittering career
After mesmerising the football world for over two decades and winning nearly every accolade, Lionel Messi announced he was done playing for Argentina, following the team's 1-0 loss to Spain in the July FIFA World Cup Final where the 39-year-old scored eight goals. The decision became clearer after his father Jorge Messi died on August 7; Messi had briefly retired after Argentina's 2016 Copa America final loss to Chile but reversed that decision and went on to win two Copa Americas and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.12_

### 2. Ailing Djokovic makes shock exit
Novak Djokovic suffered his earliest Grand Slam exit in 20 years on Sunday, beaten by Argentina's Mariano Navone 7-6 (7/5), 5-7, 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 in the US Open first round, ending his run of 78 Grand Slam first-round victories dating back to 2006. The 39-year-old vomited on court and struggled with late-match cramps and back pain, saying he has carried such physical problems "pretty much every match this year," while 46-year-old Venus Williams also lost in straight sets to compatriot Sofia Kenin 6-2, 7-6 (6).
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.15; News Trail Mangaluru p.12_

### 3. Sooryavanshi puts on a show in red ball too
Fifteen-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi blasted 92 off 81 balls to become the youngest batter to score a Duleep Trophy half-century, breaking a 57-year-old record held by the late Laxman Singh, as East Zone were bowled out for 266—identical to Central Zone's first-innings total—in the semifinal in Bengaluru on Monday. Left-arm spinner Harsh Dubey took 4 for 66 as East lost 10 wickets for 105 runs after Sooryavanshi and Abhimanyu Easwaran's 161-run opening stand, leaving Central at 45 for three at stumps in their second essay.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.15; News Trail Mangaluru p.12_

### 4. Asian Games-bound horse infected, sparks testing row
India will be down one rider-horse combination in three-day eventing at the 2026 Asian Games after Captain Aahan Kumar's horse Bolivar Gio Granno tested positive for Piroplasmosis, with reserve rider Pradeep Jhangam alleging the Equestrian Federation of India's ad-hoc committee failed to send his horse's blood sample required by August 17. Lt Col Sandeep Dewan of the ad-hoc panel disputed the allegations, saying selected riders based abroad had given undertakings that their horses were fit and that mandatory pre-quarantine testing was conducted by the official Asian Games transporter, with Bolivar Gio Granno's positive result communicated on the evening of August 25.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.15_

### 5. Shafali calls for assessment after batting implosion
India opener Shafali Verma said the team's batting unit will assess its shortcomings on turners despite opening the Women's Asia Cup with a 94-run victory over Thailand in Dubai, where India slumped from 91 for one to 159 for nine after her 64 off 36 balls. Shafali said the track offered hold and turn with the older ball and credited Thailand's bowlers, acknowledging she could have played the ball better after it gripped and arrived a little late.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.15; News Trail Mangaluru p.12_
