Headline: Shikshagraha partners with Teach for India  
Section: Bengaluru, NTB  
Body: Shikshagraha on Monday announced its partnership with Teach For India, bringing together the latter's 17 years of experience in nurturing teachers, young leaders and alumni. The partnership will add this experience to a movement that has already mobilised more than 3.9 lakh leaders across 2.1 lakh public schools. Shikshagraha is a people's movement working to strengthen one million public schools across India so that every child can learn well and be prepared for the future, it said. In a statement, Shikshagraha said the partnership brings together two complementary approaches to public education. Teach For India brings its experience in developing leaders in classrooms, while Shikshagraha contributes its growing network of more than 40 civil society organisations working with communities, governments and schools to drive collective action. Shikshagraha said it had spent the last several years turning that collective effort into measurable action, with more than 15.6 lakh micro-improvements made across public schools, led by over 3.9 lakh leaders and reaching more than 2.1 lakh schools. Of these, nearly 45,000 changes were driven.

Headline: Nepal floods: 2 from Tumakuru go missing  
Section: NT Correspondent, Tumakuru  
Body: Two US citizens originally from Tumakuru are among those reported missing following the devastating floods in Nepal, leaving their family members in Karnataka deeply worried. The missing persons have been identified as Rangusudheer, 53, and Keerthi Acharya, 50, residents of Nrupatunga Layout in Tumakuru city. Both are software engineers working in the United States. According to their family, the two had travelled to Nepal from the US on August 22 as part of a 10-member group. Rangusudheer last spoke to his relative Gurupasad over the phone at around 8.29am on August 26. Since then, the family has been unable to establish contact with either Rangusudheer or Keerthi. Saritha, Rangusududheer's sister, said the Indian Embassy had shared photographs of several bodies recovered in the aftermath of the floods and asked families to help identify them. The family is seeking information from the authorities and the Indian diplomatic mission regarding search and rescue efforts in Nepal.

Headline: KPSC scam: CID arrests middleman who made crores  
Section: NT Correspondent, Bidar  
Body: The Criminal Investigation Department has arrested Basavaraj Kannale, a Bhalki-based alleged middleman, in connection with the KPSC veterinary officers recruitment scam, opening a new line of investigation into the suspected network behind the alleged recruitment irregularities. Kannale, who originally travelled to Bengaluru to appear for KPSC examinations, allegedly later began working as a middleman for candidates seeking government jobs. Investigators are examining allegations that he used his purported political contacts and photographs with leaders of both the BJP and Congress to convince aspirants that he had influence over the recruitment process. The CID is now investigating his financial dealings, communications and links with officials connected to the recruitment process. One of the key aspects under investigation is Kannale's alleged connection with IAS officer Gyanendra Kumar. He is allegedly the owner of a house in Bengaluru and a large residential property in Bhalki and is also alleged to have purchased land worth crores of rupees in his native village.

Headline: Gruha Jyothi beneficiary survey deadline extended  
Section: NT Correspondent, Bidar  
Body: The Karnataka government has extended until September 30 the deadline for the ongoing door-to-door survey of beneficiaries under the 'Gruha Jyothi' scheme, officials said on Monday. One of the five guarantee schemes of the Congress government, Gruha Jyothi provides up to 200 units of free electricity to households. The verification survey of beneficiaries of the scheme, launched in July 2023, began on July 1. The initial deadline for completing the exercise was August 31. As of May 2026, there were 68,86,860 Gruha Jyothi beneficiaries across eight districts under the jurisdiction of Bangalore Electricity Supply Company. Of these, 46,70,588 beneficiaries, or nearly 68 per cent, had been covered under the survey as of August 31, BESCOM said. BESCOM Managing Director N Shivashankara appealed to the remaining beneficiaries to cooperate with meter readers and provide the necessary documents when staff visit their premises from September 1.
