### Final whistle: Messi calls time on glittering career

After mesmerising the football world for over two decades and winning nearly every accolade, Lionel Messi announced he was done playing for Argentina, following the team's 1-0 loss to Spain in the July FIFA World Cup Final where the 39-year-old scored eight goals. The decision became clearer after his father Jorge Messi died on August 7; Messi had briefly retired after Argentina's 2016 Copa America final loss to Chile but reversed that decision and went on to win two Copa Americas and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

### Q1 GDP grows at 7.8%, defies war impact

India's GDP expanded by 7.8% in the first quarter of the current financial year, driven by robust growth in services and manufacturing despite global disruptions from the West Asia conflict, according to official data released on Monday. The April-June growth is slower than the previous quarter's 8.6% expansion but higher than the 6.9% recorded in Q1 of 2025-26.

### SC: Won't stop Sept 5 CJP march; trust it will be peaceful

The Supreme Court on Monday declined to restrain the Cockroach Janta Party from staging a protest march in Delhi on September 5, with a bench of Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana expressing confidence the demonstration would remain peaceful. Advocate Rizwan Ahmed argued the demand was not urgent and cited police raids and international media presence; the bench said counsel could file an application if any untoward incident occurred and directed that the matter be considered by the police, referring broader issues to a High-Powered Committee.

### HC quashes Suryanagar land acquisition

In a setback to the state government, the Karnataka High Court has quashed the Karnataka Housing Board's acquisition of 1,938 acres for the Suryanagar 4th Phase Layout in Anekal, holding that the entire project falls within the eco-sensitive zone of Bannerghatta National Park. In an August 29 order, Justices DK Singh and H Shanthi Bhushan noted the government had earmarked 75 acres for a new international cricket stadium from 401.31 acres acquired in Indlavadi village, and ruled the acquisition lacked environmental impact assessment and socio-ecological considerations flagged by the Central Empowered Committee.

### Move from 'endless war': PM tells Putin

### 14% monsoon shortfall so far, Sept too will be deficient: IMD

Three months into the southwest monsoon season, all southern states remain severely rain deficient with little hope of recovery in September, which is likely to receive below-normal showers, the India Meteorological Department said on Monday, adding that August was the hottest month on record since 1901. Karnataka has a cumulative shortfall of 23%, Andhra Pradesh tops the list at 42%, Kerala and Tamil Nadu are both at 21%, Telangana at 19%, and the national deficiency is 14%, with September rainfall expected at 91% of the 167.9 mm average.
