Headline: Final whistle: Messi calls time on glittering career  
Section: GOAT ERA  
Body: After mesmerising the football world for a little over two decades and winning nearly every accolade the sport had to offer, Lionel Messi brought an end to an extraordinary career by announcing that he was done playing for Argentina.

The news had been brewing ever since Argentina lost to Spain 1-0 in the FIFA World Cup Final in July, a tournament where the 39-year-old dished out another age-defying performance with eight goals — just two shy of this generation’s superstar Kylian Mbappe.

It became even clearer when his father Jorge Messi — the person behind his talismanic rise from a kid struck by growth hormone deficiency in Rosario to one of the greatest sportspersons ever to grace the planet — passed away on August 7. A distraught Messi then revealed that even he didn’t know how much football was left in him.

Messi may have lived most of his life in Spain, starting with his association with Barcelona where he first made his mark, but Argentina was closest to his heart. Despite his club accolades, Messi couldn’t quite replicate the same with La Albiceleste during his early years. And there were constant comparisons with the enigmatic Maradona.

And when Argentina lost the 2016 Copa America final to Chile, Messi decided enough was enough. He did reverse his decision rather swiftly, and since then had been operating at a different level. He went on to win two Copa Americas and the all-important FIFA World Cup in Qatar 2022. It was the greatest day in his life, leaving no scope for deliberation on who the Greatest Of All Time of this era was.

Headline: SC: Won’t stop Sept 5 CJP march; trust it will be peaceful  
Body: The Supreme Court on Monday declined to restrain the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) from staging a protest march in Delhi on September 5, expressing confidence that the demonstration would remain peaceful.

A bench of Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana observed, “Right now, there are no compelling circumstances to presume that anything wrong will happen…”

Advocate Rizwan Ahmed, appearing for the applicant, argued that CJP’s demand was not urgent. He said the police were already conducting raids to arrest those named in FIRs. If denied, they could approach the court, he submitted.

Ahmed urged the bench to issue notice and hear the matter on September 3 or 4, citing the presence of international media and the risk of a mishap by mischievous elements.

The bench said if any untoward incident occurred, counsel could file an application. It clarified that the matter must be considered by the police and directed that the application be tagged with the main case.

Ahmed submitted that the group was acting as if the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita did not apply to them and stressed the need for permission from the police.

The bench observed that such issues were the responsibility of law-and-order enforcement agencies.

“All aspects cannot be looked into by us. Please go to the High-Powered Committee we have formed,” the Chief Justice said.

Headline: HC quashes Suryanagar land acquisition  
Section: Says notified 1,938 acres fall within Bannerghatta ESZ  
Body: In a big setback to the state government, the Karnataka High Court has quashed the acquisition of 1,938 acres of land by the Karnataka Housing Board (KHB) for the Suryanagar 4th Phase Layout in Anekal, holding that the entire project falls within the eco-sensitive zone (ESZ) of the Bannerghatta National Park.

Incidentally, the government has earmarked 75 acres to build a new international cricket stadium out of the 401.31 acres acquired for the housing project in Indlavadi village.

Housing is not the only requirement for human existence. Ecology, wildlife, forest and water streams all are equally important for human existence, a division bench of Justices DK Singh and H Shanthi Bhushan noted in their order issued on August 29.

“We are, therefore, of the view that since the project in question itself falls within the Bannerghatta National Park’s eco-sensitive zone of 268.96 sq kms, the whole project is misconceived and would have irreversible ecological, environmental and wildlife related consequences. Therefore, the entire land acquisition proceedings are nothing but an exercise without undertaking the relevant considerations such as scientific study of environment impact assessment, socio-ecological factors etc., as flagged by the CEC (apex court-appointed Central Empowered Committee),” the division bench noted in their order.

The preliminary notification for land acquisition for the project in 2013 had identified 2,220 acres and two guntas. The final notification was issued in 2015 for 1,938 acres and 13 guntas.

Several landowners had challenged the acquisition. On January 13, 2025, a single bench allowed the acquisition of agricultural lands subject to necessary clearances from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). The bench, however, quashed the acquisition of converted lands, except for the purpose of forming roads as per the comprehensive development plan.

The Russia-Ukraine war is now in its fifth year. Moscow has maintained pressure with drone and missile attacks on the cities and infrastructure of Ukraine. Kyiv, in turn, has expanded long-range strikes against targets inside Russia.

For India, the prolonged war sustains risks to energy and food security, trade, inflation and broader geopolitical stability.

Modi’s message to Putin on ending the Russia-Ukraine conflict came four years after the prime minister had told the president that today’s era had not been an “era of war”.

Separately, Modi and Pezeshkian discussed bilateral ties as well as the recent developments in the West Asia conflict. The PM expressed concern at the escalation of tensions.

Headline: 14% monsoon shortfall so far, Sept too will be deficient: IMD  
Body: Three months into the southwest monsoon season, all the southern states remain severely rain deficient with barely any hope for a recovery in September that is likely to receive “below normal” monsoon showers, the India Meteorological Department said here on Monday, adding that August was also the hottest month on record since 1901.

Andhra Pradesh tops the list of rain-deficient states with a cumulative shortfall of 42% followed by Karnataka (-23%), Kerala and Tamil Nadu (both at -21%) and Telangana (-19%). For the country, the deficiency is 14%.

The shortfall is likely to aggravate as the monthly rainfall for the nation as a whole in September 2026 is most likely to be 91% of the average rainfall of 167.9 mm. This falls into the ‘below normal’ category.

“In September, below-normal rainfall is likely in peninsular, west-central and northwest India,” Mrutyunjay Mohapatra, director general of meteorology at IMD, said.
