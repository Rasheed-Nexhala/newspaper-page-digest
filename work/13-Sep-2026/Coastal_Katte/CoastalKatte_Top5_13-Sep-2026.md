### 1. MLC seeks ₹5,000-crore special package for coastal Karnataka at Cabinet meeting
MLC and State Congress working president Manjunath Bhandary said he had sought a ₹5,000-crore special economic package for coastal Karnataka to be included in the September 18 Mangaluru Cabinet meeting agenda to improve infrastructure, fisheries, tourism, and higher education and protect coastal culture, and that Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar responded positively and asked the Chief Secretary to include prominent demands. His memorandum also sought an advanced skill development centre, a permanent solution to coastal erosion, upgrading the Brahmavara Agriculture/Horticulture Diploma College as an Agricultural University using 342 acres and ₹20 crore infrastructure, upgrading Mangaluru Fisheries College as a fisheries university, establishing a Karnataka Fisheries Council, developing a modern Aqua Park, raising compensation for fishermen's families who die while fishing from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh, completing the urban ecopark at Pilikula, developing a Karavali religious tourism circuit, and building a coastal parallel road from Talapady to Byndoor.

### 3. Vande Bharat to Bengaluru should leave in morning, say stakeholders
Patrons and associations in coastal Karnataka hope the South Western Railway runtime trial of the Vande Bharat Express between Bengaluru and Mangaluru—leaving Bengaluru in the morning and returning at night—will not set the regular service schedule, saying the train should start from Mangaluru early and return by night for people with official and business work in the State capital. G. Hanumanth Kamath of Pashchima Karavali Railway Yatri Abhivriddhi Samithi and KCCI president P.B. Ahmed Mudassar said a morning departure around 6 a.m. would mirror the popular Mangaluru–Thiruvananthapuram Vande Bharat pattern; Thursday's trial covered Yeshwantpur–Mangaluru Junction in 7.05 hours and the return in 6.49 hours, with SWR sources telling The Hindu the target is to complete both directions within six hours by speeding up sections where authorised sectional speed is 110 kmph.
