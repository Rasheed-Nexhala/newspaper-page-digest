### UNDERSTANDING EARLY FACIAL Wrinkles: Causes and prevention strategies

Premature facial wrinkles are driven largely by photoaging from UV exposure accounting for up to 80% of visible aging, plus dehydration, repetitive expressions, glycation from refined sugar, smoking, pollution, stress and poor sleep. Prevention includes daily SPF 30+ sunscreen, retinoids, vitamin C, barrier-repair moisturisers, hydration, silk pillowcases, anti-inflammatory diet and 7-9 hours of sleep, with dermatologist consultation advised for persistent concerns.

Doctors warn that painless lumps that keep growing, feel firm or fixed, exceed about 5 cm or recur after removal need evaluation because they may signal sarcoma in muscle, fat, nerves or bone. Diagnosis involves examination, ultrasound or MRI and core-needle biopsy, with surgery often central and radiation, chemotherapy or immunotherapy considered at specialist centres.
