**Date:** Monday, 19 October 2026  |  **Papers scanned:** Deccan Herald; The Hindu; News Trail

## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. Priest, accused of sexually harassing minor boys, held
Mangaluru police arrested St. Vincent Ferrer Church priest Siprian Pinto for alleged aggravated sexual assault on five 14-year-olds; after four friends described similar conduct, the victim and his parents approached the Mangaluru Bishop and, on his instructions, lodged a complaint on October 15 citing incidents from August 14. The case is registered under POCSO Sections 8 and 9 and BNS Section 75(2).
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru PDF p.4 (printed p.2) — Priest, accused of sexually harassing minor boys, held; News Trail Mangaluru p.3 — Priest held after boys allege sexual harassment_

### 2. Emergency evacuation during temple fairs crucial
Mangaluru cardiologist Dr Padmanabh Kamath urged mandatory emergency evacuation routes at crowded festivals after a man suffered cardiac arrest during Navaratri at Kudroli Gokarnanath Temple around 8.30 pm and relatives carried him on a motorcycle through the crowd; he underwent angioplasty and is stable.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 3. 690 pending applications related to birth, death certificates disposed of
MCC disposed of 690 birth and death certificate applications in a October 14–17 drive after officials returned from Special Intensive Revision duty; Commissioner Rahul Rathnam Pandey said 217 people collected certificates by appointment and others may collect from the MCC cell at the head office.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru PDF p.5 (printed p.3)_

### 4. Food safety officials raid bakery in Mangaluru
Acting on Health Minister U T Khader's instructions, district food safety officer Dr Praveen Kumar CH raided a bakery at Falnir Junction on Saturday and seized products lacking manufacture date, expiry, batch number and FSSAI licence details.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 5. Special drive sought to declare DK plastic-free district
Former MGNREGA ombudsman Sheena Shetty petitioned District In-charge Minister U T Khader at Konaje Praja Seva to enforce the plastic ban and scientific waste disposal; Khader assured he would convene officials to work toward declaring Dakshina Kannada plastic-free.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Forest Department creating hurdles in implementing KUSUM-C scheme for farmers, MP complains to Shivakumar
Udupi-Chikkamagaluru MP Kota Srinivas Poojary told CM D.K. Shivakumar that delay clearing acacia on 13, 7.5 and 10 acres in Byndoor, Brahmavara and Kapu taluks is holding up a ₹22 crore KUSUM-C solar project and risking withdrawal of 30% Central grant.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru PDF p.5 (printed p.3)_

### 2. Now, demand for a High Court bench at Udupi gains ground
A Brahmavara meeting backed establishing a coastal High Court bench in Udupi, citing travel of nearly 1,000 km to Bengaluru and a population above 50 lakh across DK, Udupi and Uttara Kannada; advocate Prem Prasad Shetty welcomed the Karnataka HC chief justice's three-member feasibility committee.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 3. Man arrested in stabbing incident
Police on Sunday arrested Ajith Kumar Shetty, 28, after Lokesh alleged he stabbed Uday over a money dispute at Pallamajalu Junction, Bantwal taluk, on Saturday night; Uday was treated at Bantwal government hospital and shifted to Mangaluru. A case was registered at Bantwal Town Police Station under BNS Sections 118(2), 109(1) and 351(2).
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 4. Prof Baragur Ramachandrappa selected for Dr Shivaram Karanth Award
Dr Shivaram Karanth Trust will present the Karanth Award to Prof Baragur Ramachandrappa at Dr B B Hegde First Grade College, Kundapur, on October 24 at 4 pm, alongside Karanth Special Awards to Dr P V Bhandary and others and a national seminar on re-reading Karanth's narratives.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 5. Ponnu Pili performers entertain audience
At Uchila Dasara at Sri Mahalakshmi Temple on Saturday, 14 teams competed in Ponnu Pili and 33 in saree categories sponsored by Mahalakshmi Cooperative Bank, with Kinni Pili winners including Viha P Suvarna (0–2 years) and Namish (4–6 years); Udupi MLA Yashpal Suvarna said women's competitions have run for four years with a separate platform for children this year.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 1. A decade on, Pink Line's UG cost hits Rs 1,000 cr/km
Bengaluru Metro's 21.26-km Pink Line cost has been revised from Rs 8,730 crore in the DPR to Rs 17,346 crore, with its 13.76-km underground section costing Rs 13,876 crore (Rs 1,008.43 crore per km). Phase 2 overall is pegged at Rs 40,425 crore pending Centre assent; the Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere elevated stretch may open soon and the Dairy Circle–MG Road–Nagavara underground section is targeted by March 2027.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Two decades on, 85% of Bengaluru's CDP roads remain on paper
Roughly 1,000 km of Comprehensive Development Plan roads earmarked since the 2007 RMP remain largely unbuilt even as large housing projects were approved citing them, including a stalled 5.5-km Panathur–Gunjur–Varthur link; a mobility expert cited in the report said about 85% of promised CDP roads are still on paper amid land-acquisition and TDR disputes.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Farmers' march enters Dharwad, they await response from Centre, state
KRRS–Hasiru Sene's sugarcane price padayatra reached Guledakoppa in Dharwad on day five despite CM Shivakumar's talks; leaders gave Centre and state another deadline and warned of mobilising 50 lakh farmers at Yerikoppa on October 22, while HC restrained protests outside Union Minister Pralhad Joshi's residence.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5_

### 4. Drought & cane price war may bring down sugar output by 30%
Belagavi officials say drought cut sugarcane area from 7.50 lakh to 7.10 lakh hectares with yield expected to fall 30%, while price disputes delay harvest; sugar production may drop by about 5 lakh tonnes as crushing falls toward 520 lakh tonnes of cane versus 576 lakh tonnes last season.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5_

### 5. Kambala debuts at Mysuru Dasara; 150 pairs of buffaloes compete
Kambala made its Mysuru Dasara debut on Sunday as Urban Development Minister Yathindra Siddaramaiah flagged off the two-day buffalo race at Sathagalli near VTU, featuring 150 pairs. Dr. Yathindra said bringing a pair to Mysuru costs around ₹5 lakh; Puttur MLA Ashok Rai credited CM D.K. Shivakumar and said the sport draws an estimated 30 lakh to 40 lakh spectators across Karnataka.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — India

### 1. Toll in Kolkata pandal stampede rises to 19
The death toll in Saturday’s stampede at the Chorbagan Sarbajanin Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata rose to 19 on Sunday, with four children and 11 women among the dead; seven people including the puja committee secretary and vice-president were arrested. Police filed a suo motu case under Section 105 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita; the West Bengal government announced ₹25 lakh compensation to kin of the deceased as CM Suvendu Adhikari ordered crowd-management steps for the rest of the festival.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. 15 killed as truck runs into devotees near M.P. temple
Fifteen people were killed and more than 40 injured after a truck laden with paddy ploughed through barricades into devotees near the Sheetla Mata temple in Gwalior around 1.30 a.m. on Sunday. CM Mohan Yadav ordered a probe, transferred the City SP, suspended eight personnel, and announced ex gratia of ₹4 lakh to kin of the deceased, ₹2 lakh for the critically injured and ₹50,000 for others; PM Narendra Modi announced ₹2 lakh per deceased and ₹50,000 per injured from the PMNRF, and the driver and owner were arrested from Dholpur.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Tiger Shroff escapes assassination bid by Bishnoi gang
Actor Tiger Shroff escaped unhurt after three motorcycle-borne men opened fire at him in Mumbai's Juhu around 7.30 pm on Sunday; his driver Firdaus and a passerby were wounded. The Lawrence Bishnoi gang claimed responsibility, and Juhu Police and Mumbai Crime Branch-CID launched investigations with 15 teams formed.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 4. 'Only on paper': SC warns of contempt action over menstrual hygiene order
Supreme Court bench of Justices J B Pardiwala and R Mahadevan gave states and UTs a final chance to report implementation of its judgment making menstrual hygiene part of Article 21 rights, threatening contempt notices to non-responsive chief secretaries and listing the matter for December 14.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.9_

### 5. Oil firms bar ethanol from sugar for blending in fuel
OMCs’ October 16 tender for 2026-27 seeks about 11 billion litres of ethanol for 20% blending but bars sugar-cane juice, syrup and B-heavy molasses per government directions, cutting maize incentive to ₹68.86 a litre from ₹71.86.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.12_

## Top 5 — International

### 1. 'Like we had a war': 1,800 arrests as violent protests roil France
France reeled on Sunday from Saturday's protests that saw 1,800 arrests, a historic town hall torched in Limoges and widespread looting in Paris, Marseille, Lyon and other cities. Authorities said about 5.5 lakh people protested nationwide while organisers counted about 8 lakh; more than 500 police, gendarmes and firefighters were injured, and President Emmanuel Macron was to address the nation.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Iran urges US to accept proposal to reopen Strait of Hormuz
Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi said Iran's seven-day Strait of Hormuz reopening proposal to the US remains on the table after mid-September talks stalled, conditioning progress on lifting port blockades, oil sanctions and regional hostilities.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_

### 3. Saudi skyscraper closes offices as Houthi disruption spreads
Riyadh's Kingdom Centre ordered mandatory remote work for office tenants Sunday amid drone interceptions and Houthi attacks; schools extended online classes and King Khalid International Airport stayed closed after a major attack killed 13 people.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_

### 4. Pak seals temple after demolition attempt
Pakistan's Evacuee Trust Property Board sealed the nearly 200-year-old Ram Leela Temple in Dera Ismail Khan on Saturday after residents stopped demolition for a commercial plaza.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_

### 5. Europe refused to sanction China, India over Russia oil: US
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the US paused Russia sanctions and related tariffs on India for 60 days under the Lindsey Graham Act after European allies declined secondary sanctions on Chinese and Indian Russian oil purchases.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12_
