## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. Traffic restrictions in view of CM's visit to Kudroli
Mangaluru City Police warned of congestion on Thursday evening as Chief Minister D K Shivakumar is scheduled to visit Kudroli Gokarnanatha Temple for Mangaluru Dasara. Traffic between New Chitra Junction and Durgamahal Junction should be avoided from 4 pm to 8 pm; Kudroli main road will not be available for vehicles or parking, and visitors were told to park in the Mannagudda–Gandhinagar area.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 2. Taxi Men's Assn sets deadline for MIA to withdraw toll fee
Dakshina Kannada Taxi Men's and Maxicab Association set a 10-day deadline for Mangaluru International Airport to withdraw the Rs 50 toll on commercial vehicles entering airport premises. Honorary president and former MLA K Raghupathi Bhat warned of an indefinite relay hunger strike if the fee is not withdrawn.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 3. Good response to MCC drive to clear birth, death certificate applications
Mangaluru City Corporation began a four-day drive (Wednesday–Saturday) to clear pending birth/death certificate applications after SIR deputation delays; about 500 applications filed July 1–September 30 are being taken up, with priority for Class 10 students, and the cell has cleared nearly 4,000 applications in two months.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_

### 4. 22-year-old loses ₹2.37 lakh in escort service fraud
A 22-year-old Mangaluru resident lost ₹2.37 lakh between October 7 and 8 after responding to an Instagram ad for “Royal Escort Girls,” transferring money via QR/UPI to an Airtel Payments Bank account for deposits and fees; a complaint was filed at the Cyber, Economic and Narcotics Crime Police Station.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

### 5. ABVP seeks action against ACP for slapping student
ABVP State joint secretary Suvith Shetty said ACP Ravish Naik slapped student Nikith during a Monday protest at St. Agnes College over Dasara exam dates (October 19 and 21) and threatened agitation if there is no apology; NSUI DK president Suhan Alva condemned ABVP interference, while Police Commissioner Sudheer Kumar Reddy said police acted to prevent a breach of peace after a college complaint.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Govt invites public suggestions/objections on renaming DK dist as M'luru
The state government proposed renaming Dakshina Kannada district as Mangaluru district and invited suggestions and objections within 30 days of Gazette publication. Additional Chief Secretary (Revenue) Rajender Kumar Kataria's October 1 notification cited public demand and administrative convenience; representations can go to the CM's Secretariat, Revenue Department, or the DK Deputy Commissioner.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 2. BSNL officials told to expedite works on towers
Udupi-Chikmagalur MP Kota Srinivasa Poojary directed BSNL officials at a progress review to upgrade facilities from 3G to 4G, maintain DG sets and batteries, and expedite new towers on 39 allotted sites. He flagged network problems at the Deputy Commissioner's office in Manipal, nearby hospitals, and tourist spots such as Kaup and Kamalashile.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 3. SWR to operate special train between Bengaluru, Karwar
South Western Railway will run Train No. 06591 Yeshvantpur–Madgaon Express Special (depart Friday 11:55 p.m.) and return 06592 Madgaon–Bengaluru Cantonment (Sunday 8:40 a.m.) for Navarathri rush, with coastal stops including Surathkal, Mulki, Udupi, Kundapura, Byndoor, Karwar and Canacona.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_

### 4. NHAI introduces mechanism to ensure clean toilets at toll plazas
NHAI will inspect toll-plaza toilets and user facilities at least weekly, issue show-cause notices for deficiencies, and declare agencies “non-performers” (barred from NHAI bids for 15 days or until fixed) if unresolved deficiencies reach 10% of plazas; a Clean Toilet Picture Challenge on the Rajamargyatra app can yield a ₹1,000 FASTag reward. Photo: Santhana/Gundmi plaza on NH-66, Udupi.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.9_

### 5. 'NITK-EMPRI can develop projects to reduce emissions'
Karnataka Net Zero Task Force vice-chairperson Upendra Tripathy said EMPRI, Bengaluru, and NIT-K Surathkal could develop west-coast pilot projects aiming to cut half a million tonnes of carbon emissions over five years, speaking at a blue-carbon/mangrove workshop at NIT-K where an MoU with EMPRI was signed.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

### 2. DGP Alok tells Chief Secy no CBI probe pending against him

_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.2_

### 3. Govt. logs record 2.04 cr. person-days since July

_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.10_

### 4. Cops arrest man accused of using fake AI accident video

### 5. KSRTC detects 4,310 ticket pilferage cases in September

## Top 5 — India

_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 2. Double-digit inflation! WPI inflation at record 11.39% in Sept
WPI inflation hit a series-high 11.39% in September from 9.92% in August on food, manufactures and fuel; food inflation rose to 8.66%. ICRA expects WPI near 12% in October; retail inflation was earlier at a 23-month high of 5.54%.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.1_

### 3. Cabinet okays IDBI Bank sale to Fairfax for ₹52,878 cr.
The Union Cabinet approved strategic restructuring of IDBI Bank with Fairfax Financial Holdings acquiring a stake for ₹52,878 crore (bid ₹81/share); government stake falls to 15% and LIC’s to 19%, billed as the largest FDI deal in India’s financial sector.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.17_

### 4. Many held as SIR protests rock capital for fifth day
Youth and student groups including Youth Congress, SFI and DYFI marched in central Delhi on Wednesday demanding scrapping of the special intensive revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, and reinstatement of Jantar Mantar as a protest site; protesters alleged beatings as police detained demonstrators on the fifth consecutive day.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 5. Centre to unveil national power data sharing framework
Power Secretary Pankaj Agarwal said the National Electricity Data Sharing Framework will roll out to enable structured power-sector data sharing, with a CEA-chaired national data governance committee.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.17_
