## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. M'luru in the running for 50-acre cricket hub
Mangaluru Urban Development Authority and Karnataka State Cricket Association are evaluating government land parcels of about 25–50 acres, including sites near Kenjar, Gurupura bridge and New Mangaluru Port Authority, for a proposed cricket stadium.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. Drunk-driving Mini Cooper crash kills 3, including M'luru youth
Three people died in Nedumbassery, Kochi, when a Mini Cooper allegedly driven under the influence by Suhail Sajeer hit a two-wheeler; a Mangaluru youth was among the victims.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Koragas stage semi-nude protest seeking title deeds
Koraga community members marched semi-nude to the Deputy Commissioner's office in Padil, Mangaluru, demanding title deeds and house sites promised earlier.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4_

### 4. AERA to hold tariff consultation for Mangaluru airport
The Airports Economic Regulatory Authority scheduled a stakeholders' meeting on October 15, 2026, on aeronautical tariffs for Mangaluru International Airport for the second control period 2024–2031.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4_

### 5. Students protest against college opposing UG semester exams during Dasara
Students of St Agnes College (Autonomous), backed by ABVP, protested undergraduate semester exams scheduled during Dasara; the college postponed papers set for October 19 and 22.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; The Hindu Mangaluru p.4_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. 'Digital arrest' scam strips farmer of ₹1.31 cr
A 52-year-old farmer from Puttur lost ₹1.31 crore to fraudsters posing as Mumbai Police and ATS officers who claimed he was linked to terrorist money laundering.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. DK child protection unit to probe sexual abuse charge against church priest
The Dakshina Kannada District Child Protection Unit is investigating allegations that a church priest sexually abused a minor girl.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.6_

### 3. Mescom chairman asks KPTCL to supply quality power
Mescom chairman K. Harish Kumar, inspecting the new 400 kV sub-station at Kadandale in Moodbidri taluk, Dakshina Kannada, on Friday, instructed KPTCL to ensure uninterrupted, quality power to the coastal region. KPTCL Superintending Engineer Chaitanya said the project aims to supply major industrial hubs and permanently address monsoon voltage fluctuations and low-voltage issues.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 4. Rare two-year-old male black leopard trapped in cage
Forest officials trapped a rare black leopard in Shirlalu village, Belthangady, and planned to release it into protected forest after a health check.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 5. Don't take part in Mysuru Dasara Kambala: KRRS
Karnataka Rajya Raita Sangha State president Badagalapura Nagendra, addressing press persons in Mangaluru on Monday, urged coastal buffalo owners registered for Mysuru Dasara Kambala on October 18 and 19 to cancel and withdraw. He cited severe drought and dry lakes, destruction of a former park site’s ecosystem including more than 50 trees, and citizens’ grievance that other Dasara events are not organised systematically.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.6_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 2. Sudha Yadav new Governor of K'taka
Senior BJP leader Sudha Yadav was appointed Governor of Karnataka, succeeding Thawar Chand Gehlot.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 3. Rs 9k cr sanctioned for Konkan Railway: Somanna
Union Minister V Somanna said ₹9,000 crore has been sanctioned for Konkan Railway works on the route serving coastal Karnataka.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1_

### 4. Difficult to meet TN's Cauvery demand: K'taka
Karnataka told the Supreme Court it cannot meet Tamil Nadu's Cauvery water demand in a rain-deficit year and asked how backlog could be cleared.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.8_

### 5. Post 42.29 lakh hearings, 33,547 voters found 'ineligible'
After 42.29 lakh hearings in Karnataka's voter-roll revision, 33,547 names were deleted as ineligible; the final list is to be published on October 27.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.6_
