### ‘Constitution mandates adequate representation’

A Karnataka High Court Division Bench of Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru and Justice K.S. Hemalekha, striking down a law that raised SC reservation from 15% to 17% and ST reservation from 3% to 7% and breached the 50% ceiling, said the Constitution mandates “adequate representation”, not “proportionate representation”. Rejecting the State’s argument that SC/ST population had grown to 24.1% while quota stayed at 18%, the Bench held that accepting population-based reservation for over 90% of Karnataka as SCs, STs and OBCs would destroy equality, found no extraordinary circumstances to breach the 50% ceiling, and noted the State had not consulted the National Commissions for SCs and STs.
<!-- /npd:article -->

### Karnataka HC strikes down law to increase quota for SCs/STs

### Contextualising WWF's report on wildlife population decline

Hannah Ritchie of Our World in Data explains that the 2026 Living Planet Index’s headline 73% average decline in studied wildlife populations since 1970 does not mean 73% of species or populations were lost or are declining. Across 35,803 populations, exactly 50% were in decline, 43% increasing and 7% stable; regional averages include a 95% decline in Latin America and the Caribbean, 80% in Africa, 49% in Asia and 23% in Europe, with trend lines shifting as more populations and only native species are included.
