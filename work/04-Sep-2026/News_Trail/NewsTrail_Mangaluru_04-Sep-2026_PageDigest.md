### SC: Bar council can't punish law students

The Supreme Court ruled Thursday that the Bar Council of India and state bar councils lack statutory authority to take disciplinary action against law students, holding such powers arise only after enrolment as advocates. A bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana clarified disciplinary control over students rests solely with their parent institutions or authorities under institutional regulations and byelaws. The court was hearing a writ petition by NALSAR alumni Mihira Sood and Abhishek Tiwari challenging BCI chairman Manan Kumar Mishra's August 13 directions to bar NALSAR's 2026 graduating batch from enrolment and order an inquiry over a campaign against the CJI; the BCI later withdrew those directions after public backlash. The bench declared the August 13 communications and any subsequent modified versions were "without any authority of law," made absolute its earlier order protecting NALSAR students and faculty from punitive action by the BCI or state bar councils, and disposed of the petition.

### Rain deficit drives rise in crop insurance enrolment

A severe monsoon deficit across Karnataka has driven a record surge in farmer enrolment for the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, with kharif registrations nearly doubling from 20,91,575 in 2025 to 37,45,804 in 2026 as the last date for enrolment ended on August 31.
