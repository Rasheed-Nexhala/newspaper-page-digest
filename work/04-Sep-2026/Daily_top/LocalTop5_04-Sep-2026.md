# Local Top 5 — 04 Sep 2026

## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. Proposal for reconstruction of Karambar bridge
The PWD sent a ₹2 crore reconstruction proposal in March 2025 after a portion of the 1963 arch bridge sank on Wednesday on the MIA–Bajpe SH-67. A temporary earthen culvert with concrete pipes allows light vehicles while heavy vehicles use alternate roads; city–airport traffic is unaffected as the bridge lies beyond MIA.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.4_

### 2. Street vendors evicted in Hampankatta
Mangaluru City Corporation officials launched an eviction drive on Thursday evening against street vendors who had illegally encroached footpaths in Hampankatta and surrounding areas, removing flowers and temporary structures and warning of stringent legal action against repeat encroachment. Dakshina Kannada Zilla Beedhibadhi Vyaparasthara Sangha Honorary President B K Imthiyaz condemned the drive, saying flower vendors from Hassan, Mandya and other districts had come for Krishnashtami and that the Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014 permits vending during festivals.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.4; The Hindu Mangaluru p.4_

### 3. Case filed against associate NCC officers of NITK over financial irregularities
Surathkal Police registered a case against NITK Associate NCC Officers Prof P Johnson and Prof H Shivananda after an internal Comptroller and Auditor General audit found TA/DA claim bills were based on false documents; the duo were detained and an internal investigation is underway. NITK Director B Ravi said the NCC unit must uphold morality and integrity, and appointed Dr Mohan Lal Meena, Assistant Professor in Chemical Engineering, as NCC caretaker officer in place of the ANOs.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.4_

### 4. 12 fishermen rescued
Two trawl boats, Mermaid and Aneed, sank after hitting sand dunes near the Mangaluru fishing harbour estuary on Thursday; 12 fishermen were rescued by other returning boats.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.4_

### 5. Railways to install double boom barrier at Pandeshwar Level Crossing
Palakkad Division, pending a road-over-bridge, will install a double boom-barrier at Pandeshwar Level Crossing at about ₹2 crore; tenders are open and work is expected to be finalised within a fortnight and completed in six months. MCC and the DC offered to widen a 200 m road stretch to four lanes when Railways widens the crossing; MLA Vedavyasa Kamath and MP Brijesh Chowta pursued the issue.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Bhatkal ASP cracks down on sand trucks
Bhatkal Sub-Divisional Assistant Superintendent of Police Ranu Gupta, IPS, has launched a crackdown on illegal sand transportation soon after taking charge, seizing six tipper trucks and detaining six persons—Harish, Ajit, Anand, Prakash, Maruti and Nazir. Police sources said three trucks were seized within Manki Police Station limits and three in Murdeshwar Police Station limits under ASP Ranu Gupta’s leadership, with cases registered at both stations and further investigation underway.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. Udupi cops arrest Cong leader, 2 others for extortion
Kota Police have arrested Congress leader Nagendra Puthran (37) of Gobbarabettu in Kotathattu village, P Sumanth Kundar (40) of Padukere in Parampalli and Sachin Shriyan (32) of Sasthana for allegedly threatening to kill a man and demanding money over bags of rice kept in his front yard under the State government’s Anna Bhagya scheme. The complainant said the three verbally abused him and threatened extortion and murder on Monday, August 31; a team led by Sub-Inspectors Praveen Kumar R and Mahantesh Jabagouda under DSP Belliyappa KU and Brahmavara Circle Inspector Gopikrishna arrested Sachin on September 1 and the other two on September 2, and all three were produced before court.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. Fraudster absconding for 14 years arrested
The North sub-division crime detection team at Moodabidri has arrested Srinivasa B R (48) of Baithadka in Jallur village, Sullia taluk, who had absconded for 14 years without appearing before court in a fraud case involving lacs of rupees. Police said he availed a vehicle loan of Rs 10 lac from Shriram Finance in 2009 to purchase a Telcon EX 70 vehicle but failed to pay instalments or produce the vehicle for inspection; a case was registered at Moodabidri police station in 2012 and the court issued a non-bailable warrant in CC No 526/2025. He was apprehended near Baithadka Junction on September 2 and produced before court; another warrant in a cheque bounce case is pending against him.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.3_

### 4. Forest staff seek divine intervention to tackle elephant menace
Belthangady forest department staff offered ‘Rangapooja’ at the Sowthadka Shree Mahaganapathi kshetra in Kokkada, praying that wild elephants would not cause loss of human life or damage to agriculture. During 2026-27, over 80 wild elephants destroyed crops and areca plantations worth over Rs 77 lakh in Belthangady, Sullia and Kadaba taluks; Rs 3.30 lakh compensation was released to 19 people while Rs 73 lakh remained pending for 381, and forest officials have submitted a proposal to establish an elephant camp at Gundya.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.4_

### 5. Khader to launch Praja Seva Andolana in Puttur tomorrow
Dakshina Kannada District In-charge Minister U T Khader will launch the ‘Praja Seva Andolana’, Chief Minister D K Shivakumar’s initiative to take government to people’s doorsteps, at the Town hall in Puttur on September 5. Praja Seva Abhiyan meetings will be held at taluk and hobli levels across the state every Saturday, with AI-based outbound calls and Gram One kiosks at venues; grievances will be handled through separate new and unresolved queues, and eligible beneficiaries will receive on-the-spot sanction letters at meetings.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.4_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 1. Cabinet withdraws Parks Amendment Bill
The Cabinet on Thursday decided to withdraw the contentious Parks bill (Karnataka Government Parks (Preservation) (Amendment) Act, 2026) by means of an ordinance, following backlash from citizens and Opposition parties. The bill, passed in the monsoon session, had amended the 1975 Act governing Bengaluru's lung spaces including Cubbon Park and Lalbagh to allow 5% park land for public purpose; Deputy Chief Minister G Parameshwara said it would be reintroduced in the next full-fledged session after modifications based on suggestions, with contentious clauses related to sale, lease, gift and mortgage to be reviewed after public consultations. The Cabinet withdrew the bill after discussing acquisition of land in Lalbagh, Bengaluru Palace and Cubbon Park for the tunnel road project; Chief Minister D K Shivakumar posted on X that the decision reflected the government's commitment to respect public opinion and reintroduce the Bill with necessary amendments.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.7; News Trail Mangaluru p.7; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. Govt. suspends Gyanendra Kumar Gangwar over KPSC irregularities
The State suspended IAS officer Gyanendra Kumar Gangwar, who was arrested and is facing a CID inquiry over alleged irregularities in the KPSC veterinary officers' exam; he served as KPSC Controller of Examination from February 2024 to March 2026 before his appointment as MSME Director, and was the first accused arrested and is in CID custody. The CID is likely to summon suspended KPSC Chairman Shivashankarappa S Sahukar again, and on Thursday conducted a spot mahazar at the Sanjay Nagar, Bengaluru residential premises of Basavaraj Kannale, one of two key suspects in custody in the alleged KPSC irregularities.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.6; News Trail Mangaluru p.7; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 3. State to hold 3-day special session on drought, Kasturirangan report
A three-day special session will be convened from September 21 to 23 to discuss the drought situation in Karnataka and the Kasturirangan Committee report. Deputy Chief Minister G. Parameshwara said about 50 to 60 taluks may be added to the drought-hit list in a second round, ₹1,229 crore approved under the State Disaster Relief Fund is yet to be released though ₹187 crore has already been spent and ₹329 crore is with Deputy Commissioners, and Leader of the Opposition R. Ashok demanded at least a week-long session. The Cabinet also cleared Invest Karnataka 2027 from April 20, a ₹249 crore development plan for University of Visvesvaraya College of Engineering on the IIT model, and ₹46 crore for trauma centres including 100 basic life support ambulances.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.5; News Trail Mangaluru p.7; The Hindu Mangaluru p.6_

### 4. Wipro to invest Rs 1,000 crore in hydraulic pump manufacturing plant
Wipro Kawasaki Precision Machinery, a manufacturer of hydraulic pumps, has come forward to establish a manufacturing plant with an investment of Rs 1,000 crore in Karnataka, state Large and Medium Industries Minister M B Patil said on Thursday. The project, proposed to be set up in Phase V of the Harohalli Industrial Area, is expected to generate employment for around 1,200 people. Japan-based Kawasaki, in partnership with Wipro, has sought 30 acres of land for the facility, with operations expected to commence within a year; the company currently manufactures 80,000 hydraulic pumps annually at a leased unit at Kumbalgodu.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2; News Trail Mangaluru p.12_

### 5. 4,500 electric buses for Bengaluru
The Cabinet cleared the Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation's proposal to add 4,500 electric buses over the next couple of years, a Union government-funded purchase at ₹1.2 crore to ₹1.5 crore per bus that significantly expands bus services in Bengaluru. The city already has the largest city bus fleet in the country, with around 7,000 buses.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.6_

## Top 5 — India

### 1. BCI has no power to punish law students expressing dissent: SC
The Supreme Court held that the Bar Council of India has no statutory power to regulate the conduct of law students, observing that educational institutions must take action against students under their own regulatory norms. A bench comprising Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana set aside two BCI notifications issued over objections by Hyderabad's NALSAR University of Law students to the CJI's proposed convocation participation, even though both were withdrawn within hours. The controversy had erupted after BCI directed state bar councils not to enrol NALSAR's 2026 graduates as advocates until further orders.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.7; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3; The Hindu Mangaluru p.10_

### 2. Govt. to spend ₹24,000 crore to modernise police force
The Union government told the Supreme Court on Thursday it has begun implementing a ₹24,000-crore umbrella Police Modernisation Mission over five years to strengthen State police and Central Armed Police Forces through greater technology use. Additional Solicitor-General Rajkumar Bhaskar Thakare made the submission in a suo motu proceeding initiated in 2025 after media reports on non-functional CCTV cameras at Udaipur police stations, as the court reviewed compliance with its 2021 judgment mandating CCTV at key station locations including lock-ups and inspectors’ rooms.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.10_

### 3. Telangana CM sacks Forest Minister Konda Surekha
Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy decided on Thursday to drop Forest and Endowments Minister Konda Surekha from the Council of Ministers with immediate effect and recommended the move to Governor Shiv Pratap Shukla, amid a row over critical remarks by her daughter against the chief minister and other party leaders. He also rescinded G Chinna Reddy's appointment as Vice Chairman of the State Planning Board and appointed Women's Commission Chairperson Gadwal Vijayalakshmi as the new VC, following confabulations with AICC leaders in Delhi after the state Congress disciplinary committee recommended tough action against Surekha and Chinna Reddy.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; News Trail Mangaluru p.1; News Trail Mangaluru p.11; The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 4. ED launches multi-State crackdown on narcotics networks across South
The Enforcement Directorate on Thursday searched 30 locations across Tamil Nadu, Keralam, Karnataka and Telangana in eight narcotics-related cases under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act as part of the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan, probing money-laundering angles in NCB seizures including methamphetamine intercepts involving Sri Lankan nationals, Chennai hotel and Ambattur hauls, a Karnataka hashish and ganja case, Telangana properties linked to Kalapathi Nithu Bai, and Keralam cases involving hybrid ganja and MDMA from Muscat.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.10_

### 5. 160 detonators found near Secunderabad Army camp
Police found at least 160 detonators of 33A size, packed 20 to a bundle, in a plastic bucket near a firing range boundary wall in Bollarum as the probe into weapons missing from the 20th Battalion of the Madras Regiment deepened on Thursday. A case was registered at the Bollarum Police Station under Sections 4 and 5 of the Explosive Substances Act against unidentified persons as investigators questioned four staff members and examined phones and social media for a possible honey-trap, believing the theft required meticulous planning and thorough knowledge of weapons.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.4; News Trail Mangaluru p.11; The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

## Top 5 — International

### 1. Jaishankar: India ready to extend 'any help' to end Ukraine war
External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar said India has consistently maintained that this is not an era of war and that solutions will not emerge from the battlefield, after meeting Ukrainian counterpart Andrii Sybiha in Kyiv. He reiterated Prime Minister Narendra Modi's statement from Bishkek two days earlier that endless war must give way to the end of war, and expressed India's support in any manner possible to end Ukraine's conflict with Russia. India has so far provided 19 consignments of humanitarian assistance to Ukraine, including 160 tonnes comprising generator sets, medicines, and medical equipment.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.1; Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.9; News Trail Mangaluru p.11; The Hindu Mangaluru p.10_

### 2. Iran targets Kuwait in retaliation for US bombardments
Iran fired at Kuwait on Thursday as part of its retaliation for US bombardments earlier in the week, with Kuwait's Ministry of Defence saying sirens sounded and air defences intercepted missiles and drones during "blatant Iranian aggression." The escalation followed US strikes on Iranian rocket launchers on an island in the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, while Israeli police separately arrested a settler suspected in a West Bank attack on Palestinians and mourners buried two Palestinians aged 16 and 19 killed by Israeli forces in Al-Mughayyir.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.9; News Trail Mangaluru p.11_

### 3. Nepal flood death toll reaches 1,259
Devastating floods on the China-Nepal border have killed at least 1,259 people in Nepal with 5,083 missing, while Chinese state media reported 21 killed in Tibet and 541 missing, taking the total toll from the August 26 disaster to 1,280 dead and 5,624 missing. Indian Ambassador Naveen Srivastava visited Syabrubesi and said Indian and Nepali teams have explored 100 metres inside the Chilime Power House tunnel using special equipment and will continue rescue work undeterred despite difficult terrain.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.11; The Hindu Mangaluru p.14_

### 4. Rubio says no India-Iran trade deal
US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he did not think India and Iran were talking of a trade deal, telling The Brian Kilmeade Show on Fox News Radio that countries develop their own foreign policy and many nations around the world talk to the Iranian regime. His remarks came in response to Prime Minister Narendra Modi's meeting with Iranian President Massoud Pezeshkian on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Summit, and Rubio said President Donald Trump has made clear that no country should help Iran evade sanctions.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.9_

### 5. 'Supersized' El Nino to be strongest ever recorded: UN
The World Meteorological Organisation said on Thursday that El Nino is set to intensify into 2027 and could be the strongest ever recorded, with near 100% likelihood of persistence through February 2027 fuelled by exceptionally warm Pacific Ocean temperatures—the first time WMO has expressed such certainty. Secretary-General Celeste Saulo said if the trajectory continues it may be stronger than anything since monitoring began, potentially shifting global rainfall and temperature patterns and fuelling droughts and typhoons as WMO steps up preparedness efforts.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.11; The Hindu Mangaluru p.14_

## Top 5 — Sports

### 1. Smriti Mandhana's 124 powers India into Asia Cup semifinals
Smriti Mandhana’s 64-ball 124 (14 fours, seven sixes) powered India to 193 for five and a 137-run win over Hong Kong in Dubai, sealing a Women’s Asia Cup semifinal berth as Hong Kong were bowled out for 56. Smriti surpassed Mithali Raj’s 10,868 runs to become the leading run-getter in women’s internationals with 10,880, went past Meg Lanning and Laura Wolvaardt for most hundreds (18) across formats, and became the first Indian woman with two T20I centuries; India next face Pakistan on September 5.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.14; The Hindu Mangaluru p.15; The Hindu Mangaluru p.15_

### 2. Anahat upsets world No. 11 to enter London Squash Classic quarters
World No. 20 Anahat Singh beat 11th-ranked Nele Gilis 13-11, 4-11, 11-8 in 39 minutes to reach the quarterfinals of the USD 144,000 PSA Gold London Squash Classic on Thursday. The reigning world junior champion, seeded eighth, will next face the winner between Malaysian world No. 5 Sivasangari Subramaniam and Egyptian world No. 32 Nardine Garas. On Wednesday, world No. 42 Ramit Tandon lost to Mohamed Elshorbagy 12-10, 11-9 in the men's second round.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.12; News Trail Mangaluru p.13; The Hindu Mangaluru p.16_

### 3. Srikanth stuns Lai, Tanvi loses the plot
Kidambi Srikanth stunned World No. 9 Victor Lai of Canada 21-18, 21-19 to enter the men's singles quarterfinals of the China Masters Super 750 badminton tournament in Shenzhen on Thursday. In women's singles, Tanvi Sharma lost 17-21, 21-18, 16-21 to World No. 9 Tomoka Miyazaki of Japan in the second round, and India's Dhruv Kapila and Tanisha Crasto bowed out 18-21, 18-21 to Taiwan's Liu Kuang Heng and Hsu Yin Hui in mixed doubles.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.13; The Hindu Mangaluru p.16_

### 4. Laxman appointed India coach for the upcoming Asian Games
V.V.S. Laxman will coach the Indian men’s team at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, later this month, assisted by Sunil Joshi, Hrishikesh Kanitkar and Subhadeep Ghosh. Laxman had guided the team to gold at the 2023 Asian Games in Hangzhou.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.15_

### 5. BCCI to prioritise rest, practice matches, acclimatisation before series in SENA nations
BCCI secretary Devajit Saikia said after a review meeting that the board will work to ensure proper rest, acclimatisation and practice matches before major series in SENA countries, citing too many matches in a year as a challenge for the next Future Tours Programme. The meeting attended by Shubman Gill, Shreyas Iyer, Gautam Gambhir and VVS Laxman also reviewed India's poor England and Ireland tours and mapped plans for the 2027 ODI World Cup.
