### Rescue efforts on a war footing in Nepal as disaster toll hits 675

Nepal's flash-flood death toll rose to 675 on Saturday as rescue operations entered a fourth day in remote areas, with at least 2,498 people still missing and more than 7,514 rescued. Among 261 tourists brought to safety were 167 Indians; over 900 people linked to 11 hydropower projects remain unaccounted for, and 279 were rescued from tunnels in two days. India sent a tunnel rescue team and six forensic expert teams, China dispatched five tunnel experts, and Prime Minister Balendra Shah said 18,708 security personnel were deployed in flood-hit areas.
