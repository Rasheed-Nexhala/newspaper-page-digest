# Local Top 5 — 30 Aug 2026
**Date:** Sunday, 30 August 2026  |  **Papers scanned:** The Hindu Mangaluru; News Trail Mangaluru

## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. R. Chethan posted as Western Range DIG, Amit Singh transferred
2010-batch IPS officer R. Chethan has been posted as Deputy Inspector General of Police, Western Range, Mangaluru, while incumbent IGP Amit Singh has been transferred to the Karnataka State Reserve Police. The government also downgraded the Western Range DIG post to match the cadre status of the DIG, Criminal Investigation Department. Mangaluru DCP (Law and Order) H.N. Mithun has been transferred and posted as Superintendent of Police, Gadag.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5; News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. MCC to launch 'Walk with Commissioner'
Mangaluru City Corporation Commissioner Rahul Ratnam Pandey will launch a monthly 'Walk with Commissioner' programme across all 60 city wards on the 16th of every month. The initiative is meant to let him inspect local problems firsthand and work toward prompt solutions for residents. The first walk is scheduled for September 16 in Ward No. 41 (Central Market).
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 3. New MUDA chairman appointed
The Karnataka government has nominated Santosh Kumar Shetty as chairman of the Mangaluru Urban Development Authority, replacing incumbent Sadashiva Ullal. The appointment, notified on Saturday, will remain in force until further orders.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 4. Get Plastic Park ready by October-end, MP tells KIADB officials
Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta asked KIADB officials at a DISHA meeting on Saturday to complete the Plastic Park at Ganjimutt for inauguration by October-end. Of 48 sites developed on 40 acres, 16 applications have been received so far, and ongoing works are nearing completion. Chowta also pressed for an action plan to saturate Koraga housing under PM-JANMAN, noted monsoon damage to arecanut crops on 18 hectares belonging to 137 farmers, and asked Southern Railway to explore a station site near Mangaluru International Airport.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 5. More measures needed to make Mangaluru railway region commuter-friendly, says MLC
Congress MLC Ivan D'Souza welcomed the Railways' decision to bring the Mangaluru region under the Mysuru Division of South Western Railway from October 1 and called for more commuter-friendly measures. He criticised BJP elected representatives for remaining silent on the move and said he was ready to join a delegation led by Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta to press Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar for improved rail connectivity. On a High Court Bench for Mangaluru, he said Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru had assured him a committee would examine the demand and would visit the region shortly.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.6_

## Top 5 — Coastal Karnataka

### 1. Dharmasthala burial case: SIT urges court to take cognisance of final report
The SIT probing the Dharmasthala mass burial case asked the Belthangady court on Saturday to take cognisance of its final report on a false complaint filed by C.N. Chinna and sought action against him and five activists. Prosecutor Divyaraj Hegde said the 7,005-page report accuses Chinna and others of multiple offences under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita for fabricating evidence related to alleged mass burials. The court posted the matter to October 3 to hear the five accused on the final report.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 2. Bikarnakatte-Sanur project 76.1% complete, says NHAI official
The four-laning of the 45.01-km Bikarnakatte (Mangaluru)–Sanur (Karkala) stretch of NH 169 has reached 76.1% completion, with 36.42 km ready, NHAI Mangaluru official Abdulla Javed Azmi told a DISHA meeting on Saturday. The ₹1,137-crore project is now expected to finish by March 2027, though a 1-km stretch near Rosa Mistica School at Gurupura-Kaikamba remains stalled under a court stay. A six-lane bridge on NH 66 across the Phalguni at Kulur is 58% complete, with one carriageway targeted for November 2026 and full completion by May 2027.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.5_

### 3. Illegal trekking, 'hidden spot' tourism raise alarm in Western Ghats
Residents and environmentalists in Kodagu and Chikkamagaluru have raised alarm over private groups and tour operators promoting remote natural spots on social media for commercial trekking without Forest Department permission. Locals warn that increased footfall is leaving plastic waste and threatening ecologically fragile forests, water sources and wildlife habitats, including areas where Neelakurinji is in bloom near Napoklu. Forest Minister Ramalinga Reddy said he has directed officials to act against illegal commercial trekking after complaints from residents, while activists in Chikkamagaluru cited off-road events and a recent incident at Rani Jhari viewpoint where tourists drove a vehicle to the edge of a nearly 3,000-ft gorge.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.7_

### 4. Meet on sensory health held
NCISM Chairperson Vaidya Manisha Kotekar inaugurated the international conference "Punarvasu" on sensory health at Dharmasthala, organised by Aptas Ayurveda Trust as part of its decennial celebrations.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 5. 'Emergency services offer promising careers'
Mangaluru Regional Fire Officer Shankar M told an awareness programme at Alva's College, Moodbidri, that fire and emergency services offer strong career prospects for science graduates and urged local youth to join the sector.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

## Top 5 — Karnataka

### 1. Lobbying for berth set to return after Nagendra's exit
With Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar accepting B. Nagendra's resignation over alleged embezzlement at the Karnataka Maharshi Valmiki Scheduled Tribes Development Corporation, two Cabinet berths are vacant and Congress leaders are lobbying to fill them. Party sources said one slot may go to a woman legislator, with Sandur MLA E. Annapurna Tukaram seen as a strong contender as a Scheduled Tribes representative from Ballari district, while Laxmi Hebbalkar, Nayana Motamma and MLC Arati Krishna are also in the race. BJP leader B. Sriramulu said the party will proceed with its 10-day Raichur-to-Ballari padayatra from September 1 despite Mr. Nagendra's resignation.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.7; News Trail Mangaluru p.2; News Trail Mangaluru p.4_

### 2. Fake medicines: Extradition of accused underway
The SIT in the Bidadi counterfeit medicine case is preparing to extradite kingpin Prashant from Azerbaijan, where customs officials have detained him and confiscated his passport. Investigators are securing a Red Corner Notice after intelligence gathered through Interpol confirmed his location abroad.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.2_

### 3. About 50 from State return home safe
About 50 people from Karnataka who were stranded after flash floods in Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region returned to Bengaluru on Saturday, while 24 from the State remain missing. The State Emergency Operations Centre said four people previously presumed missing are now in contact with authorities and can be reached at 1070 or 080-22253707. Infosys Public Services CEO Lax Gopisetty, in Nepal on a personal visit, has been unreachable since the floods, the company said.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 4. Court pulls up DCP for illegal detention
The Karnataka High Court summoned and pulled up Whitefield DCP Saidulu Adavath over the illegal detention of Mohan Gowda of Hindu Rashtra Samanvaya Samithi, who was brought to the station a day after police registered a case over his Facebook post celebrating cancellation of a Kunal Kamra show. Justice M. Nagaprasanna found that police served Gowda a notice giving three days to appear for questioning while he was already in their custody, and ordered the DGP to issue guidelines that illegal arrest or detention would attract disciplinary proceedings. The court said police cannot take citizens' liberty for granted merely because they wear a uniform.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.8_

### 5. 5 major cities to be developed to ease B'luru's burden: CM
Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar said the government will develop Mysuru, Belagavi, Hubballi-Dharwad and Kalaburagi to ease Bengaluru's population pressure and improve jobs, education and healthcare in those cities. He also outlined grand Dasara plans in Mysuru, including Kambala during the festivities and more than Rs 10 crore on illumination.
_Sources: News Trail Mangaluru p.2_
