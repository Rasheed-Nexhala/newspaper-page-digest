### NUMBER 1 — Get Plastic Park ready by October-end, MP tells KIADB officials

Number one. At a DISHA meeting on Saturday, Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta told KIADB officials to complete the Plastic Park at Ganjimutt so it can be inaugurated by October-end.

Of 48 sites developed on 40 acres, 16 applications have been received so far, and ongoing works are nearing completion.

Chowta also pressed for an action plan to saturate Koraga housing under PM-JANMAN. He noted monsoon damage to arecanut crops on 18 hectares belonging to 137 farmers. And he asked Southern Railway to explore a station site near Mangaluru International Airport.

**Host take**

October-end is the date they set for inauguration. That’s the number to watch if you follow Ganjimutt industry news.

### NUMBER 2 — Bikarnakatte-Sanur project 76.1% complete, says NHAI official

Number two. The four-laning of the 45.01-kilometre Bikarnakatte in Mangaluru to Sanur in Karkala stretch of NH 169 has reached 76.1% completion. NHAI Mangaluru official Abdulla Javed Azmi said this at the same DISHA meeting on Saturday.

Thirty-six point four two kilometres are ready. The project cost is 1,137 crore rupees. It is now expected to finish by March 2027.

But one kilometre near Rosa Mistica School at Gurupura-Kaikamba is still stalled under a court stay.

Also, a six-lane bridge on NH 66 across the Phalguni at Kulur is 58% complete. One carriageway is targeted for November 2026. Full completion is targeted for May 2027.

**Host take**

Seventy-six percent sounds close, but that one-kilometre court stay and the March 2027 date are the parts that still decide when the full stretch actually opens.

### NUMBER 3 — More measures needed to make Mangaluru railway region commuter-friendly, says MLC

Number three. Congress MLC Ivan D’Souza welcomed the Railways’ decision to bring the Mangaluru region under the Mysuru Division of South Western Railway from October 1.

He said more measures are needed to make the region commuter-friendly. He criticised BJP elected representatives for remaining silent on the move. He said he was ready to join a delegation led by Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta to press Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar for improved rail connectivity.

On a High Court Bench for Mangaluru, he said Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru had assured him a committee would examine the demand and would visit the region shortly.

**Host take**

October 1 is the date on the railway shift. The Bench visit is still “shortly,” per what the MLC said — so watch for the committee, not a court date yet.

### NUMBER 4 — Dharmasthala burial case: SIT urges court to take cognisance of final report

Number four. The SIT probing the Dharmasthala mass burial case asked the Belthangady court on Saturday to take cognisance of its final report on a false complaint filed by C.N. Chinna.

The SIT sought action against him and five activists. Prosecutor Divyaraj Hegde said the 7,005-page report accuses Chinna and others of multiple offences under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita for fabricating evidence related to alleged mass burials.

The court posted the matter to October 3 to hear the five accused on the final report.

**Host take**

I’m only reporting what the court and the prosecutor said. October 3 is the next fixed date on this report.

### NUMBER 5 — MCC to launch 'Walk with Commissioner'

Number five. Mangaluru City Corporation Commissioner Rahul Ratnam Pandey will launch a monthly Walk with Commissioner programme across all 60 city wards on the 16th of every month.

The idea is to let him inspect local problems firsthand and work toward prompt solutions for residents.

The first walk is scheduled for September 16 in Ward Number 41 — Central Market.

**Host take**

September 16, Ward 41. That’s a concrete place and date if you want to see how this programme actually starts.
