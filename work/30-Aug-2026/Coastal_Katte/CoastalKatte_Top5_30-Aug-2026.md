### 1. Get Plastic Park ready by October-end, MP tells KIADB officials
Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta asked KIADB officials at a DISHA meeting on Saturday to complete the Plastic Park at Ganjimutt for inauguration by October-end. Of 48 sites developed on 40 acres, 16 applications have been received so far, and ongoing works are nearing completion. Chowta also pressed for an action plan to saturate Koraga housing under PM-JANMAN, noted monsoon damage to arecanut crops on 18 hectares belonging to 137 farmers, and asked Southern Railway to explore a station site near Mangaluru International Airport.

### 2. Bikarnakatte-Sanur project 76.1% complete, says NHAI official
The four-laning of the 45.01-km Bikarnakatte (Mangaluru)–Sanur (Karkala) stretch of NH 169 has reached 76.1% completion, with 36.42 km ready, NHAI Mangaluru official Abdulla Javed Azmi told a DISHA meeting on Saturday. The ₹1,137-crore project is now expected to finish by March 2027, though a 1-km stretch near Rosa Mistica School at Gurupura-Kaikamba remains stalled under a court stay. A six-lane bridge on NH 66 across the Phalguni at Kulur is 58% complete, with one carriageway targeted for November 2026 and full completion by May 2027.

_Why Coastal Katte: Hard NH infra with ₹1,137cr, 76.1% progress, March 2027 / Kulur bridge Nov 2026 dates — commute-life hook, strong conversion category._

### 3. More measures needed to make Mangaluru railway region commuter-friendly, says MLC
Congress MLC Ivan D'Souza welcomed the Railways' decision to bring the Mangaluru region under the Mysuru Division of South Western Railway from October 1 and called for more commuter-friendly measures. He criticised BJP elected representatives for remaining silent on the move and said he was ready to join a delegation led by Dakshina Kannada MP Capt. Brijesh Chowta to press Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar for improved rail connectivity. On a High Court Bench for Mangaluru, he said Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru had assured him a committee would examine the demand and would visit the region shortly.

### 4. Dharmasthala burial case: SIT urges court to take cognisance of final report
The SIT probing the Dharmasthala mass burial case asked the Belthangady court on Saturday to take cognisance of its final report on a false complaint filed by C.N. Chinna and sought action against him and five activists. Prosecutor Divyaraj Hegde said the 7,005-page report accuses Chinna and others of multiple offences under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita for fabricating evidence related to alleged mass burials. The court posted the matter to October 3 to hear the five accused on the final report.

_Why Coastal Katte: Major coastal accountability story (Dharmasthala SIT) with Oct 3 court date — civic stakes, forwardable update._

### 5. MCC to launch 'Walk with Commissioner'
Mangaluru City Corporation Commissioner Rahul Ratnam Pandey will launch a monthly 'Walk with Commissioner' programme across all 60 city wards on the 16th of every month. The initiative is meant to let him inspect local problems firsthand and work toward prompt solutions for residents. The first walk is scheduled for September 16 in Ward No. 41 (Central Market).
