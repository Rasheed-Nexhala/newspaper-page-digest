### NUMBER 1 — Traffic disrupted as bridge caves in

Number one. An old bridge near the Karambar petrol pump on State Highway 67 caved in on Wednesday morning — and it is messing up traffic between Mangaluru and Bajpe, including the route many people use to reach Mangaluru International Airport.

This bridge was built in 1963. Police are telling motorists to use Jari Nagar or Porkodi roads while the PWD works on a diversion for light vehicles. All three papers we scanned today carried this story, which tells you how big the disruption is.

If you had a flight or a hospital appointment on that side of town, check your route before you leave home.

**Host take**

This is exactly the kind of infra failure that changes your commute overnight — not next year, today.

---

### NUMBER 2 — Kinnigoli-Mulki highway offers a challenge: Travel on stretch without falling into a pothole!

Number two. The Kinnigoli-Mulki state highway is in such bad shape that residents are literally asking whether you can travel the stretch without falling into a pothole.

The asphalt is worn out at multiple points. Work for the Amrut-2 drinking water pipeline cut the road in several places, and locals say it was not restored properly. Residents report four people injured on two-wheelers in just the past two days.

There is a Rs 2 crore proposal to develop the Mulki-Kinnigoli road — but it has not been sanctioned yet. If you use this road daily, ride slow and spread the word.

**Host take**

A ₹2 crore fix is on paper but not on the ground — and people are getting hurt while they wait.

---

### NUMBER 3 — Three sub-inspectors suspended over bribe-for-post conversation

Number three. Three sub-inspectors in Uttara Kannada district have been suspended after an audio clip went viral on social media.

In the roughly two-minute recording, the officers — who are friends — are allegedly heard discussing money paid to secure the Haliyal law-and-order sub-inspector posting, and about being posted in the same taluk to make money together.

Superintendent of Police M N Deepan ordered the suspension and started a departmental enquiry. The clip has been shared widely, including by the JD(S) social media cell accusing the government of corruption in police postings.

**Host take**

When the audio is out and the SP acts the same day, people pay attention — because postings and bribes hit trust in everyday policing.

---

### NUMBER 4 — 2 fishing boats capsize, ten people rescued

Number four. Two wooden fishing trawlers capsized in the estuary near Mangaluru Fisheries Harbour on Tuesday evening.

All ten labourers on board were rescued without injuries. One boat, Mermaid II owned by Shareef of Alekala in Ullal, was towed ashore. The rope pulling the second vessel, Aniq owned by Aboobakkar Azmal of Bengre Kasaba in Kuloor, snapped and that boat fully capsized.

Everyone came back safe this time — but it is another reminder of how quickly things turn at the harbour.

---

### NUMBER 5 — Schools told to strictly adhere to safety standards for vehicles

Number five. Mangaluru City Traffic Police held a school bus safety programme at Town Hall for around six hundred drivers, owners, and school representatives.

Officials told managements to follow Karnataka Motor Vehicles Rules, verify vehicle and driver documents, keep fire extinguishers and speed governors, and stay below forty kilometres per hour. DCP Ravi Shankar K warned that legal action will follow violators.

If your child rides a school van or bus in Mangaluru, this is worth asking the school about directly.

**Host take**

Parents should not assume every bus on the road meets these standards — ask, check, and share if something looks off.
