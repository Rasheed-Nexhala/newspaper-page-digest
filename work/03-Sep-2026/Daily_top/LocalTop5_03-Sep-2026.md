## Top 5 — Mangaluru

### 1. Traffic disrupted as bridge caves in
Traffic was disrupted on SH 67 after an old small bridge near the Karambar petrol pump caved in on Wednesday morning; Bajpe police advised motorists to use Jari Nagar or Porkodi roads as alternative routes while the PWD works on a diversion for light motor vehicles. Vehicular movement between Mangaluru and Bajpe on Mangaluru-Athradi State Highway 67 was affected after an old minor bridge built in 1963 at Karambar, between MIA and Bajpe, became unstable; passengers travelling from Bajpe towards Mangaluru International Airport have been advised to take alternative routes following the temporary closure of a road near Karambaru.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3; News Trail Mangaluru p.3_

### 2. 2 fishing boats capsize, ten people rescued
Two wooden fishing trawlers capsized in the estuary near Mangaluru Fisheries Harbour on Tuesday evening but all 10 labourers aboard were rescued without injuries; one boat was towed ashore while the rope pulling the second snapped and it completely capsized. The vessels were Mermaid II, owned by Shareef of Alekala, Ullal, and Aniq, owned by Aboobakkar Azmal of Bengre Kasaba, Kuloor.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 3. Schools told to strictly adhere to safety standards for vehicles
The Mangaluru City Traffic Police organised an awareness programme on school bus safety at Town Hall for around 600 drivers, owners and school representatives, instructing managements to adhere to Karnataka Motor Vehicles Rules, 2012, verify vehicle and driver documents, ensure fire extinguishers and speed governors, and keep speeds below 40 kmph. DCP (Crime and Traffic) Ravi Shankar K warned that legal action would be taken against violators.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2_

### 4. MCC warns of action against unauthorised street vendors
The Mangaluru City Corporation directed persons vending without valid identity cards to voluntarily remove their activities, warning that failure would invite legal action under the Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014, following complaints of unauthorised vending obstructing footpaths and traffic. MCC asked street vendors carrying out vending without authorisation to voluntarily vacate public places, warning that if they failed to do so the corporation would evict them using force.
_Sources: Deccan Herald Mangaluru p.2; The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_

### 5. Private medical colleges bound to provide services free of cost, says Khader
Health Minister U.T. Khader expressed strong displeasure over the lackadaisical approach of private medical colleges in providing services at government healthcare centres, saying colleges are bound to provide services free of cost. He told reporters in Mangaluru on Wednesday that if they do not abide by conditions, the State government will cancel the Memorandum of Understanding and inform the National Medical Commission. At a recent review meeting he found colleges not providing doctors and other services required under MoUs meant to fill personnel vacancies and provide specialised treatment at government hospitals; he cited Community Health Centre in Ullal as a fine example where a private institution provided four MBBS doctors, twice-a-week specialist clinics and 10 nurses, while three private medical colleges in Dakshina Kannada signed MoUs for Bantwal Taluk Hospital, Moodbidri CHC and Vitla CHC, and a private hospital is yet to start at Belthangady Taluk Hospital.
_Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.2_

The poor condition has reportedly caused several motorists, particularly two-wheeler riders, to sustain injuries; work related to the Amrut-2 drinking water pipeline further worsened the situation as the road was cut at several places and allegedly not restored properly. Large potholes have developed at stretches including near S. Kodi, Sukananda Shetty Circle, the bus stand turn, Kinnigoli Rajangana, Hosakaveri Katte, Moorkaveri, Padmanuru and Kerekadu; residents said four people suffered injuries after falling from two-wheelers over the past two days, and Rs 2 crore proposed for development of the Mulki-Kinnigoli road has not yet been sanctioned.

### 5. Elaborate security arrangements made for Vitla Pindi in Udupi
The district police have made elaborate security and traffic arrangements for the Vitla Pindi/Krishna Janmashtami celebrations in Udupi, deploying senior officers, 186 constables, 50 Home Guards, a KSRP platoon, nine DAR platoons, fire and emergency teams, ambulances and drone teams.
