### 1. Rs 30L special grant gets road from Jeppu Shrine towards Netravati, started
Road development from the vicinity of Jeppu Guligajja Daivasthana in Attavara Ward 55 of Mangaluru City Corporation to the Netravati river will proceed with a special grant of ₹30 lakh, after guddali puja under MLA Vedavyasa Kamath. Kamath said the main road touching the national highway and leading to Guligajja Katte, plus a drain, aim to end severe waterlogging during rains, following requests from local people and former corporation member Shailesh B. Shetty, with the Public Works Department taking up the work; he added that development grants are lower than under the previous BJP government but funds for this project are being arranged from the limited state Congress grant.

### 2. MLA orders evening cop cover at Darbe Circle
Puttur Congress MLA Ashok Rai has ordered a police arrangement near Darbe circle from 3 p.m. to 4.30 p.m., when St. Philomena College, the high school, Bethany School and Little Flower School let out and students scramble for buses amid moving traffic. After visiting the circle on Saturday evening and hearing from rickshaw drivers, shop owners and parents, Rai said personnel will be posted for children's safety and directed the municipality to shift the auto stand backward, demolish an old building there, and make provision for small stall-shops on the site.

### 3. Mangaluru Railway Region transition: Mysuru, Palakkad officials to meet this week
Divisional Railway Managers of Mysuru (SWR) and Palakkad (SR) divisions will meet in Mangaluru on Thursday to finalise the transition of the Mangaluru Railway Region from Palakkad to Mysuru, effective October 1 per an August 21 Railway Ministry order. SWR jurisdiction will extend to Ullal (inclusive) from Padil and till Thokur (exclusive) north — about 30 km of the 39 km route to Mysuru — covering roughly 2,000 railwaymen; Mangaluru Central handles about 30 arrivals/departures and the Junction about 70 halting plus 10 originating trains, with Central housing a Palakkad coaching depot of about 17 pairs and ~450 coaches, and Panambur Yard handling NMPA freight.

_Scope: mangaluru_ · _Sources: The Hindu Mangaluru p.3_
_Why Coastal Katte: Railway region shift from Oct 1 changes how Mangaluru commutes and connects — deadline-driven daily-life impact._

### 4. MCC Commissioner inspects infra projects
Mangaluru City Corporation Commissioner Rahul Ratnam Pandey inspected the proposed multi-level car parking facility at the old bus stand on K S Rao Road under the PPP model, where MSCL General Manager Arun Prabha said around 84 landowners had not agreed to part with properties and the old bus stand is more than seven decades old with a proposal to lease land for 30 years. Pandey directed officials to submit a month-wise report for the next 15 months within a week, and at the Surathkal-Idya wet well he directed water samples be sent for laboratory testing after residents complained of groundwater contamination affecting local wells.

### 5. KSRTC bus catches fire
A KSRTC Sarige bus travelling from BC Road to Bengaluru via Subrahmanya was gutted after catching fire near Kaikamba on the Kaikamba-Gundya road in Bilinele late on Saturday night, but all 30-35 passengers were evacuated safely after the BC Road depot crew noticed the fire and alerted them. KSRTC officials from the Puttur division inspected the spot and initiated an inquiry as the exact cause is yet to be established.
