### Mangaluru Railway Region transition: Mysuru, Palakkad officials to meet this week

Divisional Railway Managers of Mysuru (SWR) and Palakkad (SR) divisions will meet in Mangaluru on Thursday to finalise the transition of the Mangaluru Railway Region from Palakkad to Mysuru, effective October 1 per an August 21 Railway Ministry order. SWR jurisdiction will extend to Ullal (inclusive) from Padil and till Thokur (exclusive) north — about 30 km of the 39 km route to Mysuru — covering roughly 2,000 railwaymen; Mangaluru Central handles about 30 arrivals/departures and the Junction about 70 halting plus 10 originating trains, with Central housing a Palakkad coaching depot of about 17 pairs and ~450 coaches, and Panambur Yard handling NMPA freight.
