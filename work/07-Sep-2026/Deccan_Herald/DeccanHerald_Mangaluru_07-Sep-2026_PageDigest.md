### B'luru has 4,800-km-long problem

As much as 4,800 km of Bengaluru's 10,000-km water supply pipelines were laid nearly 70-80 years ago, and 57 of the city's 83 Ground Level Reservoirs are 40-70 years old, causing frequent leakages, repeated repairs and road damage, a senior BWSSB official said. In the 240 sq km core area alone, 4,800 km of pipeline is 70-80 years old; another 2,400 km in seven CMCs and one TMC added in 2007 is 10-15 years old, and 2,800 km in 110 villages added to BBMP in 2007 is 1-2 years old, with old cast iron pipes in some cases allowing sewage to enter leaks and mix with water.

### Over 22 lakh affected by floods in Bihar
